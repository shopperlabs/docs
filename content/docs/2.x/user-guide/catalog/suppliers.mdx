Suppliers represent external vendors or manufacturers who provide products for your store. This is primarily used in **dropshipping** scenarios where products are shipped directly from the supplier to the customer, without you handling inventory yourself.

<Note>
The supplier feature must be enabled by your developer before it appears in the admin panel. If you don't see "Suppliers" in the sidebar, ask your developer to enable it (see [Feature Flag](/2.x/suppliers#feature-flag)).
</Note>

## Accessing Suppliers

When the feature is enabled, suppliers appear under the Products section:

1. Click on **Products** in the sidebar
2. Select **Suppliers** from the submenu

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/suppliers-list.png" alt="Suppliers list page" />

## Adding a Supplier

To add a new supplier:

1. Click the **New supplier** button
2. Fill in the supplier details
3. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/suppliers-create.png" alt="Add supplier form" />

### Supplier Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Yes | The supplier's company name |
| **Email address** | No | Contact email for placing orders |
| **Phone number** | No | Contact phone number |
| **Contact** | No | Primary contact person at the supplier |
| **Website** | No | Supplier's website URL |
| **Visibility** | No | Whether the supplier is active and can be picked on products. Off by default. |
| **Description** | No | In the **Description** section, details about the supplier and their products |
| **Notes** | No | In the **Description** section, internal notes (not visible to customers) |
| **Metadata** | No | In the **Metadata** section, extra key and value pairs your developer can use |

## Editing a Supplier

To update a supplier's information:

1. Click the **Edit** icon next to the supplier
2. Modify the fields you want to change
3. Click **Save**

## Enabling or Disabling a Supplier

The **Visibility** column in the list shows whether each supplier is active; it cannot be clicked. To change it, edit the supplier and switch **Visibility**, or select several suppliers in the list and use the **Enable** or **Disable** bulk action. Disabled suppliers stay linked to their products but no longer appear in the product **Supplier** dropdown.

## Linking Products to Suppliers

Products are linked to suppliers from the product edit form, once the product is created: the creation wizard has no supplier field. When editing an **External** product:

1. Go to the product's edit page
2. In the **Overview** tab, find the **Supplier** dropdown in the **Product information** section, next to **Product external Id**
3. Select the supplier. Only enabled suppliers are listed
4. Fill in **Product external Id**, the identifier of the product at your supplier. Both fields are required
5. Click **Update**

The **Supplier** dropdown, like the **Suppliers** menu, only appears when the supplier feature is enabled.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/suppliers-product.png" alt="Supplier field on product form" />

<Tip>
Only **External** products can be linked to suppliers. Standard, variant, and virtual products are managed from your own inventory.
</Tip>

## How Dropshipping Works

When a customer orders a product linked to a supplier, the order flow is:

1. **Customer places order** on your store
2. **You receive the order** in the admin panel
3. **You forward the order** to the supplier (via email, their portal, or API)
4. **Supplier ships directly** to the customer
5. **You add tracking info** from the supplier to the order
6. **Customer receives the product**

The key difference from regular orders is that you never handle the physical product. The supplier ships directly to your customer.

<Warning>
With dropshipping, you depend on the supplier's inventory and shipping speed. Make sure to verify your supplier's reliability before listing their products. Delays or quality issues reflect on your store.
</Warning>

## Supplier Information to Collect

When onboarding a new supplier, gather this information:

| Information | Why You Need It |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Order email** | To send purchase orders when customers buy |
| **Product catalog** | To know what they offer and at what price |
| **Lead time** | To set customer expectations for delivery |
| **Return policy** | To handle customer returns |
| **Payment terms** | To manage your cash flow (Net 30, prepaid, etc.) |
| **Minimum order** | To know if there's a minimum quantity per order |
