After creating a product, you can manage all its details from the product edit page. This guide covers the common management tasks available for all product types.

## Product Edit Page

Click on any product from the products list to open its edit page. The page is organized into tabs based on your product type: **Overview**, **Media**, **Pricing** (all types except Variant), **Files** (Virtual), **Attributes** (Standard, Virtual and Variant), **Variants** (Variant), **Stock & Inventory**, **Shipping** (Standard and Variant), **SEO** and **Related Products**.

## Pricing

The pricing section lets you configure your product's price, comparison price, and cost. If your store supports multiple currencies, you can set prices in each currency.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-pricing.png" alt="Product Pricing" />

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Price amount** | The regular selling price shown to customers. Required when you fill in **Compare at price**. |
| **Compare at price** | The original price displayed with a strikethrough to indicate a discount. Leave empty if the product is not on sale. |
| **Cost per item** | Your cost to acquire or produce the item. Used for profit margin calculations and not visible to customers. |

Click **Add new price** to open the **Product pricing** panel, fill in the fields and click **Save**. The prices you set are listed per currency in the **Product pricing** table. To change a price, click **Edit** on its row: the panel then shows only that currency.

### Multi-Currency Pricing

If you have multiple currencies configured in your store:

- Each currency has its own set of price fields in the **Product pricing** panel
- The **Add new price** button only shows while at least one of your store currencies has no price yet
- Set appropriate prices for each market (don't just convert; consider local pricing strategies)
- The customer sees the price in their local currency based on your store's currency detection

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-add-pricing.png" alt="Multi-Currency Pricing" />

<Tip>
  For variant products, pricing is set at the variant level. The parent product doesn't have its own price. Each variant can have a different price.
</Tip>

### Pricing Strategies

| Strategy | How to configure |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Fixed price** | Set only the "Price amount" field |
| **Sale price** | Set "Price amount" lower than "Compare at price" |
| **Cost-plus** | Fill "Cost per item" to track margins, set "Price amount" with your desired markup |
| **Anchor pricing** | Set a high "Compare at price" to make the actual price feel like a deal |

## Media Management

Manage your product's visual content, including thumbnail and gallery images.

### Thumbnail

The thumbnail is your product's primary image. It appears in:
- Product listings and search results
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Order confirmations and invoices

### Product Gallery

Additional images shown on the product detail page:
- Upload multiple images for different angles
- Upload them in the order you want them shown, as the gallery has no drag and drop reordering
- Delete images you no longer need

**Image recommendations:**
- Minimum resolution: 800x800 pixels
- Consistent aspect ratio across products (square works best for most stores)
- Clean white or neutral background for the thumbnail
- Lifestyle and detail shots for gallery images

## SEO Optimization

Optimize your product pages for search engines to drive organic traffic.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-seo.png" alt="Product SEO" />

| Field | Description | Best practice |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Title** | The title shown in search engine results | Keep under 60 characters. Include the product name and a key benefit. |
| **Description** | The meta description shown below the title in search results. Limited to 160 characters. | Describe the product and include a call to action. |
| **Metadata** | Extra key and value pairs saved with the product. Click **Add row** to add a pair. | Only fill it in if your storefront is built to read these keys. Ask your developer which ones it uses. |

Click **Update** to save. Next to the form, a preview shows how the product can appear in search results, including its URL. The URL ends with the product slug, which is created from the product name when you create the product and cannot be edited. Choose a clear product name from the start, since renaming the product later does not change the slug.

### SEO Tips

- **Include keywords** naturally in the title and description. Think about what customers search for
- **Be specific**. "Red Leather Crossbody Bag" is better than "Nice Bag"
- **Add a call to action** in the meta description, such as "Shop now", "Free shipping", "Limited edition"
- **Avoid duplicate content**. Each product should have unique SEO text

<Note>
  If left empty, search engines will use your product name as the title and pull text from your description. It's always better to set these explicitly for optimal results.
</Note>

## Related Products

Link related products to encourage customers to explore more of your catalog and increase average order value.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/related-product.png" alt="Related Products" />

### Adding Related Products

1. Open the **Related Products** tab on your product. Its section is titled **Similar Products**
2. Click **Add product** (or **New product** if the product has no related products yet)
3. In the **Add Similar Products** panel, search for published products, select them and click **Add Selected Products**
4. Related products appear on the product detail page on your storefront

To unlink a product, click **Remove** on its card and confirm.

### When to Use Related Products

| Use case | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Complementary items** | Shoes: shoe cleaner, laces, socks |
| **Same category** | Blue dress: other dresses in similar style |
| **Frequently bought together** | Phone: phone case, screen protector |
| **Upsell** | Basic model: premium model of the same product |
| **Accessories** | Camera: lens, tripod, bag |

## Visibility & Publishing

Control when and where your product is visible. Both settings are in the **Product availability** section of the **Overview** tab.

### Visibility Toggle

- **Visible**: Product appears on your storefront and in search results
- **Hidden**: Product exists in the admin but is not shown to customers

Use hidden mode to:
- Prepare products before a launch
- Temporarily remove seasonal items
- Hide products being updated or photographed

### Publication Date

The **Availability** date controls when the product goes live:

- **Past or current date**: Product is immediately available (if visible)
- **Future date**: Product is scheduled and will become available at that date and time

<Tip>
  Combine a future publication date with "Visible" to schedule product launches. The product won't appear until the scheduled date.
</Tip>

## Channels

If your store uses multiple sales channels, you can control which channels a product appears on with the **Sales channels** field, in the **Associations** section of the **Overview** tab. It lists the enabled channels of your store, for example:

- **Web store**. Your main online store
- **Mobile app**. If you have a mobile shopping app
- **POS**. Point of sale / in-store
- **Marketplace**. Third-party marketplace listings

Each product can be assigned to one or more channels.

## Common Actions

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Edit product info** | Click on the product, modify fields, click **Update** |
| **Change visibility** | Toggle the **Visible** switch in the **Overview** tab, click **Update** |
| **Update stock** | Go to the **Stock & Inventory** tab, click **Add stock** |
| **Update pricing** | Go to the **Pricing** tab, click **Edit** on a price, save |
| **Delete product** | Click **Delete** at the top of the product page, or on its row in the products list (irreversible) |
| **Duplicate product** | There is no duplicate action. Click **New product** and fill in the details again |

<Warning>
  Deleting a product cannot be undone from the admin. It also removes the product's images, prices and stock history. Consider hiding the product instead if you might need it again.
</Warning>
