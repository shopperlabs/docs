---
title: Creating a Product
description: Step-by-step guide to creating a product using the creation wizard.
---

Creating a product in Shopper is done through a guided wizard that walks you through each step. The process is the same for all product types, with some steps adapting based on the type you choose.

## Starting the Wizard

From the Products page, click the **New product** button to open the product creation wizard.

## Step 1: Choose the Product Type

The first step asks you to select the type of product you want to create. Each type is presented as a card with an icon and a description to help you make the right choice.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-add-step-type.png" alt="Choose Product Type" />

The available types are:

- **Standard**. Physical products that require shipping
- **Virtual**. Digital products (downloads, subscriptions, courses)
- **External**. Dropshipping products fulfilled by a third party
- **Variant**. Products with multiple options (size, color, material)

<Tip>
  If your store only sells one type of product, select it and turn on **Set as default product type** below the cards. The type is saved for the next products you create, and the wizard then opens directly on the General Information step. You can still go back to this step to pick another type or turn the toggle off. Changing this toggle requires access to the store settings.
</Tip>

Select your product type and click **Next** to continue.

## Step 2: General Information

This step collects the core information about your product.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-add-step-info.png" alt="Product General Information" />

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Yes | The product name displayed to customers. A URL-friendly slug is generated automatically. |
| **Slug** | Yes | Filled in from the name. You cannot type in this field. |
| **Summary** | No | A short description shown in product listings and cards. |
| **Description** | No | The full product description with rich formatting (bold, lists, links, etc.). |
| **Visible** | No | On by default. Turn it off to hide the product from all sales channels. Useful to prepare products before launching. |
| **Availability** | Yes | The date and time when the product becomes available. It defaults to now and cannot be earlier than one hour ago. Pick a future date to schedule the publication. |

<Note>
  The **Name** field automatically generates a URL slug (e.g., "Blue Running Shoes" becomes `blue-running-shoes`). The slug cannot be edited, and it stays the same if you rename the product later.
</Note>

## Step 3: Associations

Link your product to the organizational structures of your store. This step only appears when your store uses brands, categories or collections.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-add-step-relations.png" alt="Product Associations" />

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Brand** | Associate the product with a brand. Only enabled brands are listed. Hidden if your store does not use brands. |
| **Categories** | Assign one or more categories from the category tree. You can select a parent category or any of its subcategories. Only enabled categories are listed. |
| **Sales channels** | Choose which sales channels this product appears on (e.g., web store, mobile app, POS). Only enabled channels are listed, and your default channel is selected for you. |
| **Collections** | Add the product to one or more collections. |

<Tip>
  You can always update associations after creation. If you haven't set up categories or brands yet, you can skip this step and come back later.
</Tip>

## Step 4: Media

Upload images to showcase your product.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-add-step-4.png" alt="Product Media Upload" />

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Thumbnail** | The main image shown in product listings, search results, and cards. Upload one image. |
| **Images** | Additional images shown on the product detail page. Upload multiple images for different angles or details. |

**Image best practices:**
- Use high-resolution images (minimum 800x800px recommended)
- Keep a consistent aspect ratio across products
- Show the product from multiple angles
- Use a clean background for the thumbnail

## Step 5: Stock & Inventory

Set up the initial inventory information for your product.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/product-add-step-5.png" alt="Product Stock & Inventory" />

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **SKU** | No | Stock Keeping Unit, a unique identifier for your product in your inventory system. Two products cannot share the same SKU. |
| **Barcode** | No | The product barcode (UPC, EAN, ISBN, etc.) for scanning and identification. Letters, numbers and hyphens only, unique per product. |
| **Quantity** | No | The initial stock quantity available for sale. It is added to your default inventory location. |
| **Safety Stock** | No | The minimum stock level you want to keep before reordering. Shopper saves this number with the product but does not send a notification when stock reaches it. |

<Note>
  This step also appears for **Variant** products. What you enter here applies to the parent product. Each variant (per size/color combination) gets its own SKU, barcode and stock, which you set when you create the variants after saving.
</Note>

<Note>
  This step also appears for **Virtual** and **External** products. You can leave it empty when there's no physical stock to manage.
</Note>

## Completing the Creation

After filling in all the steps, click **Save** to create your product. You'll be redirected to the product detail page where you can:

- Configure pricing
- Set up shipping dimensions (Standard & Variant products)
- Add product attributes (Standard, Virtual & Variant products)
- Upload digital files (Virtual products)
- Generate variants (Variant products)
- Optimize SEO settings
- Link related products
