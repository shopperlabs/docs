Staff users are the people who help manage your store. Shopper allows you to create multiple admin accounts with different levels of access.

## Overview

The Staff management section allows you to:

- Add new administrators
- See every staff account with its role and level of access
- Assign roles and permissions
- Delete the accounts of people who no longer need access

## Accessing Staff Settings

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Staff & permissions** from the settings menu

The page opens with the title **Administrators & roles**. The **Administrators accounts** table lists your staff:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Full Name** | The staff member's name and registration date. Your own row shows a **Me** badge |
| **Email** | Login email address, with a green icon once it is verified |
| **Role** | The role assigned to the staff member |
| **Access** | **Full** for administrators, **Limited** for every other role |

## Adding a New Admin

To add a new staff member:

1. Click the **Add administrator** button
2. Under **Login information**, enter the **Email address** and a **Password**, or click **Generate** to create one. Turn on **Send Invite** to email the login details to the new administrator
3. Under **Personal Information**, enter the **First name** and **Last Name**, and optionally the **Gender** and **Phone number**
4. Under **Role Information**, pick a role in **Choose a role for this admin**
5. Click **Save**

The account can be used right away: its email address is marked as verified when you create it.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/add-admin.png" alt="Add Administrator" caption="Add Administrator" />

### Admin Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Email address** | Yes | Login email address (must not be used by another account) |
| **Password** | Yes | Login password. **Generate** fills in a random 16-character password, and the eye icon shows it |
| **Send Invite** | No | Sends the new administrator an email with their login information |
| **First name** | Yes | Staff member's first name |
| **Last Name** | Yes | Staff member's last name |
| **Gender** | No | Male or Female |
| **Phone number** | No | Contact phone number |
| **Choose a role for this admin** | Yes | The role of this user, picked from a list of radio buttons |

<Note>
Only administrators can assign the **Administrator** role to a new team member. For other staff members, this role does not appear in the role choices. A warning in the form reminds you that the Administrator role gives the same rights and permissions as yours.
</Note>

## Staff Roles

Each staff member is assigned a role that determines what they can access:

| Role | Typical Access |
|------|----------------|
| **Administrator** | Full access to all features. Installed with Shopper |
| **Manager** | Installed with Shopper without any permission: give it the ones your managers need, for example everything except settings |
| **Editor** | A role you create for product and content management |
| **Support** | A role you create for orders and customer management |

See [Roles & Permissions](/2.x/user-guide/administration/roles) to create roles and choose their permissions.

<Tip>
Create roles that match your team structure. The principle of least privilege suggests giving users only the access they need for their job.
</Tip>

## Managing Existing Staff

### Edit Staff Member

The staff list has no edit action: you cannot change another person's name, email address, password or role after the account is created. Instead:

- Each staff member updates their own details and password from their profile (see [Your Profile](#your-profile) below)
- To change what someone can do, change the permissions of their role on the [Roles & Permissions](/2.x/user-guide/administration/roles) page. The change applies to everyone with that role

### Disable Staff Member

Shopper has no option to disable a staff account and enable it again later. To stop someone from signing in, delete their account as described below. If they come back, add them again with **Add administrator**.

### Delete Staff Member

Permanently remove a staff account:

1. Click the trash icon on the staff member's row in **Administrators accounts**. The same icon appears on the **Users** tab of each role
2. Confirm the deletion

- Only administrators see the trash icon, and it never appears on an administrator's row
- Use with caution - this cannot be undone

## Your Profile

Each staff member can manage their own profile. Click your avatar at the top-right of the header, then **Personal Account**:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/profile.png" alt="User Profile" caption="User Profile" />

From your profile, you can:

- Update personal information
- Change your password
- Enable two-factor authentication, when it is turned on for your store
- See the devices signed in to your account and log out your other browser sessions

## Security Recommendations

1. **Remove inactive accounts** - Delete the accounts of staff who leave
2. **Use role-based access** - Don't give everyone admin access
3. **Enable two-factor** - Add an extra layer of security
4. **Monitor activity** - Ask staff to check the devices signed in to their account from their profile and log out the sessions they don't recognize. Shopper does not keep a history of staff actions
5. **Regular password changes** - Encourage periodic password updates
