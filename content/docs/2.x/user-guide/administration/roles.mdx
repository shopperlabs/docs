Roles and permissions allow you to control what each staff member can do in your store's administration panel. This ensures security and prevents unauthorized access to sensitive areas.

## Overview

The access control system consists of:

- **Roles** - Groups of permissions assigned to users
- **Permissions** - Individual actions a user can perform

## Accessing Roles & Permissions

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Staff & permissions**

The page opens with the title **Administrators & roles**. Your roles appear as cards under **Administrator role available**, each with its number of accounts and a **View details** link that opens the role.

## Understanding Permissions

Permissions control access to specific actions:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/permissions.png" alt="Permissions" />

### Permission Categories

Permissions are sorted into groups. Most groups hold the same five permissions for one area of the admin: **Browse** (see the list), **Read** (open a record), **Edit**, **Add** and **Delete**.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **System** | **Access Dashboard**; **Access Setting** (all store settings except staff, roles and permissions); **Views Users** (staff accounts, roles and permissions) |
| **Brands** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete brands |
| **Categories** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete categories |
| **Collections** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete collections |
| **Products** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete products, plus the same five permissions for inventories, attributes, reviews, product variants, suppliers and tags |
| **Customers** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete customers |
| **Orders** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete orders. There is no separate permission for refunds |
| **Discounts** | Browse, read, edit, add and delete discounts |

Permissions created without a group are listed under **Custom permission**.

### Browsing Permissions

Open a role with **View details** and click the **Permissions** tab. Every permission is listed by group, with a checkbox that shows whether the role has it and the date it was created:

## Managing Roles

### Creating a Role

To create a new role:

1. On the **Administrators & roles** page, click the **+** button next to **Administrator role available** (its tooltip reads **Add new role**)
2. Enter the **Name (in lowercase)**, for example "manager"
3. Enter the **Display name** shown in the admin, for example "Manager", and a **Description**
4. Click **Save**
5. Open the new role with **View details**, click the **Permissions** tab and check the permissions the role needs. Each checkbox is saved as soon as you click it

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/add-role.png" alt="Add Role" />

### Role Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Name (in lowercase)** | Internal name of the role, unique (e.g., "store_manager") |
| **Display name** | Name shown in the admin (e.g., "Store Manager") |
| **Description** | What this role is for |

Permissions are not part of this form: you check them on the role's **Permissions** tab once the role exists.

### Updating a Role

Open the role with **View details**. The role page has three tabs:

- **Role**: change the **Name (in lowercase)**, **Display name** and **Description**, then click **Update**. Editing the name fills the display name for you. On the Administrator role, a warning reminds you that a wrong change could block your access to the dashboard
- **Users**: the staff members who have this role
- **Permissions**: check or uncheck the permissions of the role

A role you created can be removed with the **Delete** button at the top of its page. The Administrator and Manager roles that Shopper installs cannot be deleted.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/update-role.png" alt="Update Role" caption="Update Role" />

Changes apply immediately to all users with that role.

## Creating Permissions

Add custom permissions for your specific needs from a role's page. Two buttons at the top of the page, shown to **Administrator** accounts only, create permissions and give them to that role straight away:

- **Create permission** opens the **New permission** window. Pick a **Group name** (optional), enter the **Permission name (in lowercase)** (up to 30 characters, unique), the **Display name** (up to 75 characters) and a **Description**, then click **Save**
- **Generate permissions** creates the five standard permissions (browse, read, edit, add and delete) for one area in one go. Enter the **Resource name**, for example "shipments", pick a **Group name** if you want, check the list under **Permissions that will be created**, then click **Generate permissions**

A new permission only controls something once your developer checks it in the admin or in a custom screen. Creating it does not restrict anything on its own.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/add-permission.png" alt="Add Permission" caption="Add Permission Form" />

<Note>
Creating or deleting a permission requires an **Administrator** account. Other staff members can still grant existing permissions to roles, but only permissions they hold themselves, and they cannot modify the Administrator role.
</Note>

## Custom Permissions

Create permissions tailored to your business. Permissions created without a group appear under **Custom permission** on the role's **Permissions** tab. A permission made with **Create permission** has a trash icon that lets an Administrator delete it; the permissions Shopper installs and those made with **Generate permissions** cannot be deleted.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/custom-permissions.png" alt="Custom Permissions" />

## Common Role Examples

### Store Manager

Full operational access:
- All product permissions
- All order permissions
- All customer permissions
- **Access Setting** only if they manage the store settings, as it opens all of them at once

### Content Editor

Focus on catalog management:
- View/edit products
- View/edit categories
- View/edit collections
- No order or customer access

### Support Agent

Customer service focus:
- Browse and read orders
- Browse and read customers
- Edit orders only if they handle order changes
- No product management

### Marketing

Promotional access:
- View products
- Manage discounts
- Manage collections
- No customer data access

## Permission Hierarchy

<Tip>
Some permissions may depend on others. For example, to edit a product, a user typically also needs the **Browse** permission to reach the product list.
</Tip>

When assigning permissions, consider:

1. **Browse** - Can see the list
2. **Read** - Can open a record and see its details
3. **Add** - Can add new items
4. **Edit** - Can modify existing items
5. **Delete** - Can remove items (most sensitive)

## Security Considerations

- **Know who has access** - The **Users** tab of each role lists its members. Shopper does not keep a history of who changed what
- **Separation of duties** - Don't give one person all permissions
- **Regular reviews** - Remove unnecessary access
- **Test roles** - Verify permissions work as expected
