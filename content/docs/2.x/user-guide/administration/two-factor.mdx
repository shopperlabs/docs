Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your administrator account. Even if someone discovers your password, they won't be able to access your account without the second factor.

## What is Two-Factor Authentication?

2FA requires two forms of verification:

1. **Something you know** - Your password
2. **Something you have** - A code from your phone

This means an attacker would need both your password AND your phone to access your account.

## Enabling Two-Factor Authentication

### Step 1: Access Security Settings

1. Click your avatar at the top right of the header
2. Select **Personal Account**
3. Find the **Two Factor Authentication** section

<Note>
The section only appears when two-factor authentication is turned on for your store. If you do not see it, ask your developer to turn it on.
</Note>

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/two-factor-section.png" alt="Two-Factor Section" />

### Step 2: Start Setup

1. Click **Enable Two Factor**
2. In the **Confirm Password** window, enter your password and click **Confirm**

Two-factor authentication is turned on right away. The section now shows a QR code and your recovery codes.

### Step 3: Scan QR Code

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone:
   - Google Authenticator
   - Authy
   - Microsoft Authenticator
   - 1Password

2. Open the app and scan the QR code displayed on screen. The QR code is shown only now: to use a second device, scan it with that device too before you leave the page

3. The app generates a 6-digit code for your store

### Step 4: Verify Setup

Shopper does not ask for a code at this point. Before you leave the page, check that your authenticator app shows a 6-digit code for your store: from your next login, you need it to sign in.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/two-factor-code.png" alt="Two-Factor Code" />

### Step 5: Save Recovery Codes

Below the QR code, Shopper shows 8 recovery codes. **Save these securely!**

Each recovery code lets you sign in once if you lose your phone. To see them again later, click **Show Recovery Codes** in the **Two Factor Authentication** section. **Regenerate Recovery Codes** replaces all of them with new ones.

<Tip>
Store your recovery codes in a safe place, like a password manager or a secure physical location. Don't store them on your phone.
</Tip>

## Logging In with 2FA

After enabling two-factor authentication:

1. Enter your email and password as usual
2. The **Authenticate Your Account** screen asks for a code
3. Open your authenticator app
4. Enter the 6-digit code in the **Code** field
5. Click **Login**

To use a recovery code instead, click **Use a recovery code**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/two-factor-authentication.png" alt="Two-Factor Login" />

## Disabling Two-Factor Authentication

If you need to disable 2FA:

1. Click your avatar at the top right, then **Personal Account**
2. Find the **Two Factor Authentication** section
3. Click **Disable**
4. If the **Confirm Password** window opens, enter your password and click **Confirm**

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/two-factor-disable.png" alt="Disable Two-Factor" />

<Tip>
Only disable 2FA temporarily if necessary. Re-enable it as soon as possible to maintain account security.
</Tip>

## Recovery Options

### Lost Your Phone?

If you've lost access to your authenticator:

1. **Use a recovery code** - On the login screen, click **Use a recovery code** and enter one of your saved codes
2. **Contact your developer** - No screen in the admin turns off another administrator's 2FA, so your developer turns it off for your account
3. **Use backup device** - If you scanned the QR code with a second device during setup

### Recovery Codes

Each recovery code can only be used once. After using a code:

- That code is no longer valid
- Shopper replaces it with a new one: click **Show Recovery Codes** in your profile to save the updated list
- Click **Regenerate Recovery Codes** if you think someone else has seen your codes

## Troubleshooting

### Code Not Working?

- **Check the time** - Your phone's time must be accurate
- **Try the next code** - Codes refresh every 30 seconds
- **Use a recovery code** - If you can't generate valid codes

### Locked Out?

1. Try using a recovery code
2. Ask your developer to turn off 2FA for your account
3. As a last resort, contact technical support

For developer details, see the [Two-Factor Authentication documentation](/2.x/two-factor).
