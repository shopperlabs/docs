To manage your store, you need to log in to the Shopper administration panel. This guide will walk you through the login process.

## Accessing the Login Page

Your Shopper administration panel is accessible at your website URL followed by `/cpanel`. For example:

```
https://your-store.com/cpanel/login
```

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/login.png" alt="Shopper Login Page" />

## Logging In

The login card reads **Sign in with email**.

1. Enter your email in the **Email address** field
2. Enter your password in the **Password** field
3. Optionally check **Remember me** to stay signed in on this browser
4. Click the **Login** button

<Tip>
If you've enabled two-factor authentication on your account, you'll be prompted to enter a verification code after submitting your credentials. Learn more about [Two-Factor Authentication](/2.x/user-guide/administration/two-factor).
</Tip>

## Forgot Your Password?

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Click the **Forgot your password?** link next to the **Password** label
2. Enter your email address and click **Send password reset mail**
3. Check your email for a password reset link
4. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password

## After Logging In

Once logged in, you'll be taken to the **Dashboard**, which provides an overview of your store's activity. From here, you can navigate to any section of the administration panel using the sidebar on the left.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/dashboard.png" alt="Shopper Dashboard" />

## Logging Out

To log out of your account:

1. Click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner of the header
2. Select **Sign out** from the dropdown menu

The same menu opens your profile (**Personal Account**) and the staff list (**Manage Users**, shown only if you can view staff accounts), and switches the admin between **Light**, **Dark** and **System** themes.
