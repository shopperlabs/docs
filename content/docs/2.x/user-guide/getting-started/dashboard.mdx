The Dashboard is your command center for managing your online store. Let's explore its key elements and learn how to navigate efficiently.

## Overview

When you log in, you'll see the main dashboard which displays key metrics and quick access to important features.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/dashboard.png" alt="Shopper Dashboard" />

## Main Navigation

The sidebar on the left side of the screen is your main navigation tool. It provides access to all sections of your store management:

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Dashboard** | Overview of your store's performance and recent activity |
| **Products** | Manage your product catalog |
| **Categories** | Organize products into categories |
| **Collections** | Create curated product collections |
| **Brands** | Manage product brands |
| **Customers** | View and manage customer accounts |
| **Orders** | Process and track orders |
| **Discounts** | Create and manage discount codes |
| **Reviews** | Moderate customer reviews |
| **Settings** | Configure your store settings |

## Quick Actions

From the dashboard, you can quickly access common tasks:

- Check your **Total Revenue** for the month and your **Total Products**, **Total Orders** and **Total Customers**, each compared with last month. **View more** on a card opens the matching list.
- Follow your revenue over time in the **Performance** chart
- See your **Top Selling Products**
- View recent orders in **Recent Orders**, or click **View all**
- Access store settings

On a new store, the dashboard shows a **Setup guide** instead: add your first product, create a collection, set up shipping zones, payment methods and taxes. Click **Dismiss** to hide it.

## Settings Panel

Click on **Settings** in the sidebar to access all configuration options for your store.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/settings.png" alt="Settings Panel" />

The settings panel shows one card per area, in this order:

- **[General](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/general-settings)** - Store name, contact details, address, currencies and social links
- **[Staff & permissions](/2.x/user-guide/administration/users)** - Administrator accounts, roles and permissions
- **[Inventories](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/locations)** - The places where you stock inventory and sell products
- **[Payment methods](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/payment-methods)** - The payment methods offered to your customers
- **[Carriers](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/carriers)** - Shipping carriers and delivery providers
- **[Legal](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/legal)** - Privacy policy, terms of use, shipping and refund policies
- **[Zones](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones)** - Shipping, payment and fulfillment per zone
- **[Taxes](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/taxes)** - How your store charges taxes
- **[Currencies](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/currencies)** - Enabled currencies and exchange rates

Once you open a settings page, the same areas appear as tabs at the top of the page, so you can move from one to another without going back.

## Your Profile

Access your personal profile settings by clicking on your avatar at the top-right of the header, then **Personal Account**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/profile.png" alt="User Profile" />

From here you can:

- Update your personal information
- Change your password
- Enable two-factor authentication, when it is turned on for your store
- See the devices signed in to your account and log out your other browser sessions

To sign out, click your avatar and select **Sign out**.
