When a customer places an order on your store, it appears in the **Orders** section of your admin panel. From here, you manage everything: confirm payment, prepare shipments, track delivery, and handle issues.

## Order List

Click **Orders** in the sidebar to see all your orders. The list has the columns **#** (order number), **Date**, **Status**, **Price amount**, **Purchased** (the ordered items), and **Customer**. The **Status** column shows a payment badge and a shipping badge, or a single **Cancelled** or **Archived** badge, so you see where the order stands at a glance. The **Currency**, **Zone**, and **Channel** columns are hidden by default; turn them on from the column selector above the table.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/orders.png" alt="Orders list with status badges" />

### Tabs

The tabs at the top filter orders by their current state:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **All** | Every order in your store |
| **Open** | New and processing orders that still need attention |
| **Paid** | Orders where payment has been received |
| **Fulfilled** | Orders that have been shipped or partially shipped |
| **Cancelled** | Orders that were cancelled |
| **Archived** | Orders you've archived |

### Filters

Click the filter icon to narrow down your list. You can filter by order number, customer, order status, payment status, shipping status, date range, zone, channel, or currency.

## Order Detail

Click the eye icon (**View details**) on an order to open its detail page. The page is split into two areas: the main content on the left and customer information on the right.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/order-detail.png" alt="Order detail page" />

### Header

The header stays visible as you scroll. It shows:

- The **order number**
- Three **status badges** for order status, payment status, and shipping status
- **Action buttons** for processing the order (see [Actions](#actions) below)
- **Previous / Next** arrows to navigate between orders
- The **order date**, **customer name** (linked to their profile), and **sales channel**

### Main Content

The left side of the page contains three sections:

**Fulfillment**. A visual progress bar showing where the order is in the fulfillment process (**New**, **Processing**, **Shipped**, **Completed**). If there are items that haven't been assigned to a shipment yet and the order is not completed, cancelled, or archived, a **Create Shipping Label** button appears here.

**Products**. The list of items in the order with their thumbnail, name, quantity, fulfillment status badge, and price.

**Summary**. Payment status, payment method, subtotal, shipping carrier and fee, and the order total.

### Sidebar

The right side shows:

- **Customer profile**. Name, avatar, and a link to their full profile. Below that, how long they've been a customer and how many orders they've placed.
- **Contact information**. Email and phone number.
- **Shipping address**. The delivery address for this order.
- **Billing address**. Displayed separately, or **Same as shipping address** if it matches the shipping address.
- **Private notes**. A text field where you can leave internal comments. Only your team can see these.

## Order Status

Every order has three independent statuses that track different aspects of the order.

### Order Status

The overall state of the order.

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **New** | Blue | Order just came in, hasn't been looked at yet |
| **Processing** | Purple | You're actively working on this order |
| **Completed** | Teal | Everything is done, paid and delivered |
| **Cancelled** | Red | Order was cancelled |
| **Archived** | Gray | Order removed from active list |

### Payment Status

Tracks whether you've received the money.

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Payment pending** | Yellow | Waiting for payment |
| **Authorized** | Blue | Funds are held on the customer's card but not yet collected |
| **Paid** | Green | Payment received |
| **Partially refunded** | Orange | Some of the amount has been refunded |
| **Refunded** | Gray | Full amount refunded |
| **Voided** | Red | Payment was cancelled before any money was collected |

<Note>
**Authorized** means the payment provider confirmed the customer has funds, but you haven't captured (collected) them yet. This is common for physical goods where you want to verify stock before charging.
</Note>

### Shipping Status

Tracks where the physical goods are.

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Unfulfilled** | Yellow | No items have been shipped yet |
| **Partially shipped** | Blue | Some items shipped, others are still waiting |
| **Shipped** | Indigo | All items have been shipped |
| **Partially delivered** | Purple | Some packages arrived, others still in transit |
| **Delivered** | Green | Everything has been delivered |
| **Partially returned** | Orange | Some items were returned |
| **Returned** | Gray | All items were returned |

## Actions

The actions available depend on the current state of the order. They appear in the header, some as standalone buttons, others in the **More actions** dropdown.

### Capture Payment

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/order-capture-button.png" alt="Capture payment button" />

Appears when the payment status is **Authorized**. Clicking it charges the customer for the authorized amount. A confirmation dialog will appear since this action cannot be undone.

After capture, the payment status changes to **Paid** and the button disappears.

### Archive

Appears when the order is not completed and not paid. Moves the order to the Archived tab.

<Warning>
Archiving an order affects your revenue reports. Use this for orders that will never be fulfilled, like spam or test orders.
</Warning>

### More Actions

The dropdown contains context-sensitive actions:

| Action | When it appears | What it does |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Start processing** | Order is **New** | Changes order status to Processing. The items' fulfillment status does not change |
| **Mark as paid** | Payment is **Pending** or **Authorized** | Changes payment status directly to Paid (without going through the payment provider). A **New** order also moves to Processing |
| **Mark complete** | Order is **Processing** and payment is **Paid** | Changes order status to Completed |
| **Cancel Order** | Order can be cancelled | Cancels the order and records the cancellation date |

## Fulfillment

Fulfillment is the process of getting the ordered items to the customer. Shopper tracks this at two levels: the **order level** (shipping status) and the **item level** (fulfillment status per product).

### Creating a Shipment

When an order has items that haven't been shipped yet, the **Create Shipping Label** button appears in the fulfillment section.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/order-fulfillment.png" alt="Fulfillment section" />

Clicking it opens the **Create Shipping Label** panel, where you:

1. **Choose the Carrier Service**. Pick one of your enabled carriers. The field starts with the carrier of the shipping option the customer chose
2. **Add tracking info** (optional). Enter a **Tracking number** and a **Tracking URL**
3. **Check the Items to fulfill**. Every item not yet in a shipment is listed with its product, quantity, and price. Remove the items that are not in this package with the trash icon; at least one item must stay

The right side of the panel shows the order summary and the shipping address. Click **Save** to create the shipment.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/order-create-shipping-label.png" alt="Create shipping label panel" />

Once created, the shipment appears in the **Shipments** page with the status **Label created**, and the selected items are assigned to it. Their fulfillment status stays **Awaiting fulfillment** until the carrier picks up the package.

### Partial Shipments

You don't have to ship everything at once. If an order has 5 items and only 3 are ready, create a shipment with those 3 items. The remaining 2 stay as unfulfilled and you can create a second shipment later.

The shipping status updates automatically from the shipment events (see [Adding Tracking Events](#adding-tracking-events)), not when you create the shipment:
- A shipment is **Picked up** while other items are not shipped yet: **Partially shipped**
- Every item is in a shipment that was picked up: **Shipped**
- Some items are delivered: **Partially delivered**
- Every item is delivered: **Delivered**

When the first shipment is picked up, a **New** order also moves to **Processing**.

## Shipments

The **Shipments** page gives you a dedicated view of all shipments across your orders. Access it from the sidebar under Orders. Each row shows the **Shipment ID**, the latest **Shipment Event**, the **Status**, **Shipped at**, the **Order Number**, and the **Carrier Service**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/shipments.png" alt="Shipments page" />

### Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| **All** | Every shipment |
| **Label created** | Shipments with labels created but not yet picked up |
| **In transit** | Shipments that are picked up, in transit, at a sorting center, or out for delivery |
| **Delivered** | Shipments that reached their destination |
| **Delivery failed** | Shipments where delivery failed |

### Shipment Detail

Click the **Manage shipment** icon (sliders) on a shipment to open its detail panel. To change only the carrier service or the tracking details, use the pencil icon (**Edit**) instead. The detail panel shows:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/shipment-detail.png" alt="Shipment detail panel" />

**Tracking progress**. A 5-step visual tracker:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Label created">
    Shipping label generated, waiting for carrier pickup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Picked up">
    Carrier has collected the package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In transit">
    Package is on its way to the destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Out for delivery">
    Package is with the local delivery driver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivered">
    Package has been delivered to the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Shipment details**. Shipped date, received date, and tracking number (linked to tracking URL if provided).

**Products**. The items included in this shipment with their fulfillment status.

**Timeline**. A chronological log of every event for this shipment. Each event records the status, date, location, and an optional description.

### Adding Tracking Events

Click **Add event** in the shipment detail panel to log a new tracking update. The **Status** list only offers the statuses that can follow the current one (after **Label created**, for example: **Picked up** or **Returned**). Set **Occurred at**, which cannot be earlier than the last event, and optionally add a **Location** and a **Description**. This is useful when you receive updates from your carrier and want to keep the timeline accurate. Once a shipment is **Returned**, no status can follow and the **Add event** button disappears.

Recording **Picked up** marks the shipment's items as shipped and updates the order's shipping status. Recording **Delivered** has the same effect as **Mark as delivered** below.

### Marking as Delivered

When a shipment is **Out for delivery**, a **Mark as delivered** button appears on its row in the **Shipments** table. Click it and confirm. This updates the shipment and its items to Delivered. When every item of the order is delivered and the order is **Processing**, the order automatically moves to **Completed**.

## Typical Order Flow

Here's how a standard order moves through the system:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order placed">
    A customer completes checkout. The order appears as **New** with payment **Pending** and shipping **Unfulfilled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment received">
    Depending on your payment method, the payment status changes to **Paid** (automatic capture) or **Authorized** (manual capture). If authorized, you capture it when ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start processing">
    Click **Start processing** to indicate you're preparing the order. The order moves to **Processing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create shipment">
    Click **Create Shipping Label**, check the carrier service, add tracking info, and keep the items to ship. The shipment starts as **Label created**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track delivery">
    Add tracking events as the carrier provides updates. When you record **Picked up**, the shipping status changes to **Shipped** (or **Partially shipped** if some items are not in a shipment yet). The shipment timeline shows the package's journey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery confirmed">
    Mark the shipment as delivered. Once every item is delivered, the order status automatically changes to **Completed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Abandoned Carts

Sometimes customers add items to their cart but leave without completing checkout. Shopper tracks these as abandoned carts, giving you visibility into potential lost sales.

A cart is considered abandoned when it has items but no activity for a configurable period (default: 60 minutes).

### Viewing Abandoned Carts

To see abandoned carts:

1. Go to **Orders** in the sidebar
2. Click **Abandoned Carts**, listed under **Orders**. It opens its own page

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/ug-abandoned-carts.png" alt="Abandoned carts list" />

The abandoned carts list shows each cart's number (**#**), the **Customer** (or **Guest** for shoppers who were not logged in), the number of **Items**, the **Currency**, the **Channel**, when the cart was created (**Created At**), and when it was last active (**Last Activity**). You can filter the list by customer, creation date, or channel.

### Cart Details

Click the eye icon (**View details**) on an abandoned cart to view its contents. The detail panel shows when the cart was created, the customer's name and email (or **Guest**), the channel, the items with their quantity and price, and the shipping and billing addresses if the customer entered them.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/ug-abandoned-cart-detail.png" alt="Abandoned cart detail" />

### What You Can Learn

Abandoned carts help you understand:

- **Which products are being abandoned** to identify pricing or UX issues
- **How far customers got in checkout** to improve your checkout flow: with the starter kit checkout, a cart with a shipping address got past the address step
- **Whether logged-in customers abandon more than guests** to target recovery efforts

<Tip>
Look for patterns in abandoned carts. If many customers abandon carts with the same product, the price may be too high or the shipping cost may be surprising at checkout.
</Tip>

### Automatic Cleanup

Shopper includes a cleanup task that deletes unfinished carts not updated for 30 days, to keep your database lean. It runs automatically once your developer schedules it, and they can change the 30-day threshold in the [Cart configuration](/2.x/cart#pruning).

For developer details on querying abandoned carts and building recovery email flows, see the [Cart documentation](/2.x/cart#abandoned-carts).

For developer details, see the [Orders documentation](/2.x/orders).
