Your customers are essential to your business. Shopper helps you manage customer information, track their orders, and build lasting relationships.

## Overview

The Customers section allows you to:

- View all registered customers
- Access customer details and order history
- See customer addresses
- Send login credentials to the customers you create
- Create customers manually
- Anonymize a customer who asks for their data to be removed

## Accessing Customers

Click on **Customers** in the sidebar to access customer management.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/customers.png" alt="Customers List" />

## Customer List

The customer list displays:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Full Name** | Customer's full name and picture |
| **Email address** | Contact email address |
| **Country** | Country of the customer's first address |
| **Order Placed** | Number of paid orders that are processing or completed |
| **Registered At** | When they registered |

You can filter the list by **E-mail Verified** and by registration date.

### Empty State

If you don't have any customers yet, you'll see a helpful prompt:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/customers-empty-state.png" alt="Customers Empty State" />

## Customer Details

Click a customer's name, or the eye icon (**View**), to open their profile. The header shows their name, their email (with an icon when the email is verified), how long they have been a customer, and the **Anonymize customer** button. Below it, the **Profile**, **Addresses**, and **Orders** tabs:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/customer-information.png" alt="Customer Information" />

### Profile Information

The **Profile** tab is read-only. It has two cards:

- **Profile**. First name, last name, picture, email address, birth date, and gender (when set)
- **Account**. **Email Marketing** shows whether the customer agreed to receive marketing emails, and **Two-Factor Authentication** whether they turned it on. Each shows **Enable** or **Disable**

The profile has no account status and no notes field. The verified icon next to the email tells you the customer confirmed their email address, and the phone number appears on each address card. For internal comments, use the private notes on each order.

### Addresses

The **Addresses** tab shows each of the customer's addresses as a card, labelled **Shipping Address** or **Billing Address**, with the name, street, postal code, city, country, and phone number. Addresses cannot be edited from the admin. When you create a customer, the **Address** section of the **New customer** form records their first address, which becomes their default shipping address:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/customer-address.png" alt="Customer Addresses" />

Customers can have multiple addresses. With the starter kit, they add and remove them from their account on your storefront and choose:
- **Default shipping address**
- **Default billing address**
- **Additional addresses**, kept for later orders

### Order History

The **Orders** tab lists all orders placed by the customer:

- Order numbers and dates
- Order totals
- Payment and shipping status
- Purchased items

## Creating a Customer

To manually add a customer:

1. Click the **New customer** button on the Customers page
2. Fill in the customer details
3. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/create-customer.png" alt="Create Customer" />

### Customer Fields

The form has four sections: **Profile overview**, **Security**, **Address**, and **Notifications**.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **First name** | Yes | Customer's first name |
| **Last Name** | Yes | Customer's last name |
| **Email address** | Yes | Unique email address |
| **Phone number** | No | Contact phone number |
| **Gender** | No | Male or Female |
| **Password** | Yes | Account password. Click **Generate** to fill in a random 12-character password |
| **Confirm Password** | Yes | The same password again |
| **Street Address** | Yes | First line of the customer's address |
| **Apartment, suite, etc.** | No | Second line of the address |
| **City** | Yes | City |
| **State** | No | State or region |
| **Postal / Zip code** | Yes | Postal code |
| **Country** | Yes | Country |
| **Phone number** (address) | No | Phone number for this address |
| **Customer agreed to receive marketing emails.** | No | Records the customer's consent, shown as **Email Marketing** on their profile |
| **Send customer credentials.** | No | Emails the customer their email address and password |

The customer's email is marked as verified on creation, and you return to the customer list.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/customer-create.png" alt="Customer Creation" />

## Customer Notification

The admin sends customers one email: their login credentials. When you create a customer, the **Notifications** section of the form has two checkboxes:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/customer-notification.png" alt="Customer Notification" />

With **Send customer credentials.** checked, the customer receives a welcome email ("Welcome to" followed by your store name) with their email address and password, a **Browse the website** button, and a reminder to change the password after logging in. **Customer agreed to receive marketing emails.** sends nothing: it only records consent.

Other emails customers expect do not come from the admin:
- Order confirmations: not sent by Shopper; your developer adds them to your store
- Shipping updates: not sent by Shopper; your developer adds them to your store
- Marketing emails: sent from your own email marketing tool, to customers whose profile shows **Email Marketing** enabled
- Password resets: sent by the starter kit's login pages, along with the email verification message

## Customer Account (Storefront)

Customers can manage their own accounts on your storefront. With the starter kit, their account page welcomes them by first name and links to **Orders**, **Addresses**, and **Profile**:

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/starterkit-account.png" alt="Customer Account" />

They can:
- Update personal information (first name, last name, gender, and email) under **Profile**
- Manage addresses under **Addresses**: add, remove, and set a default shipping or billing address
- View order history under **Orders**
- Change password and turn on two-factor authentication in the **Security** settings

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/starterkit-addresses.png" alt="Customer Addresses" />

## Customer Authentication

Customers register and log in through your storefront. With the starter kit, they create an account with their last name, first name, email address, and password (**Create account**), then log in with their email address and password (**Log in**). The login page also offers **Remember me** and a **Forgot your password?** link:

## Customer Data Privacy

If a customer requests their data to be removed (GDPR "right to be forgotten"), you can anonymize their account:

1. Go to the customer's profile page
2. Click **Anonymize customer**
3. Confirm with **Yes, anonymize**

This replaces the customer's personal information (name, email, phone, avatar) with anonymous data, clears their birth date and marketing consent, and deletes all their addresses. Order history is preserved for your records, but the customer's identity is removed.

<Warning>
Anonymization cannot be undone. The customer's personal data is permanently replaced. Only use this when a customer explicitly requests data removal.
</Warning>
