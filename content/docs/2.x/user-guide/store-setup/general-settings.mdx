The General Settings page allows you to configure your store's basic information. This is one of the first things you should set up when starting with Shopper.

## Accessing General Settings

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **General** from the settings menu

The page opens with the title **Store Setting**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/setting-general.png" alt="General Settings" />

## Store Information

### Store Details

Configure the basic information about your store:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Store name** | The name of your store as it will appear to customers (required) |
| **Email address** | The main contact email for your store, used by customers who need to reach you (required) |
| **Phone number** | Contact phone number for customers who want to call you (optional) |

### Assets

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Logo** | Your store logo, available to your storefront. JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 1 MB |
| **Cover photo** | A cover image for your store. Same formats and size limit as the logo |

### Store Address

Set your store's legal name and physical address. This information may be used for:

- Invoice generation
- Shipping calculations
- Legal compliance

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Legal name of the company** | The registered name of your business (required) |
| **About** | A description of your store, written in a rich text editor (optional) |
| **Street Address** | Street and number (required) |
| **City** | City (required) |
| **Postal / Zip code** | Postal code (required) |
| **State** | State, region or province (optional) |
| **Country** | Country, picked from a searchable list (optional) |

### Store Currency

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Currencies** | The currencies your products are sold in. Pick at least one. Only the currencies enabled on the [Currencies](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/currencies) settings page are listed |
| **Default currency** | The main currency displayed on your store, chosen among the currencies selected above (required) |

### Social Links

Add the full URL of your **Facebook**, **Instagram** and **Twitter** pages so customers can find your official accounts. Each field is optional.

## Store Initialization

When you first set up Shopper, you'll go through an initialization wizard that helps you configure the essential settings.

### Step 1 - Store Information

The **Information** tab, titled **Step 1 - Shop information** on screen. Fill in the **Store name** and **Email address**, then the **Country** (optional), the **Currencies** your store sells in and the **Default currency**, and click **Next**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/initialization-step-1.png" alt="Initialization Step 1" />

### Step 2 - Address Setup

The **Address** tab, titled **Step 2 - Address information** on screen. Fill in the **Street Address**, **Postal / Zip code** and **City**, and optionally the **State** and **Phone number**, then click **Next**. This address also becomes your default inventory location.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/initialization-step-2.png" alt="Initialization Step 2" />

### Step 3 - Social Links

The **Social Links** tab, titled **Step 3 - Social links** on screen. Add the full URL of your **Facebook**, **Instagram** and **Twitter** pages if you want to, then click **Save** to finish the setup and open the dashboard.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/initialization-step-3.png" alt="Initialization Step 3" />

## Saving Changes

After making any changes to your settings:

1. Review your changes
2. Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the form
3. A confirmation message will appear when your changes are saved

<Tip>
Make sure to keep your store information up to date, especially your contact email, as this is how customers and the system will reach you.
</Tip>

For developer details, see the [Setup Store documentation](/2.x/setup-store).
