Payment methods define how customers can pay at checkout. You can offer credit card payments via Stripe, cash on delivery, bank transfers, or mobile money, and restrict each method to specific [zones](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones).

## Accessing Payment Methods

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Payment methods** from the settings menu

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/payment-methods-list.png" alt="Payment methods list" />

The payment methods list shows all configured methods with their driver, status, and last update date.

## Adding a Payment Method

To add a new payment method:

1. Click the **Add payment method** button
2. Fill in the payment method details
3. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/payment-methods-create.png" alt="Add payment method form" />

### Payment Method Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Provider** | Yes | The name shown to customers at checkout (e.g., "Credit Card", "PayPal"). Shopper creates the method's identifier from it |
| **Driver** | Yes | The payment provider that processes this method. Defaults to **Manual** for offline payments; choose a provider such as **Stripe** for online processing |
| **Provider Documentation URL** | No | External URL for the payment provider |
| **Additional details** | No | Shown to customers when they choose a payment method |
| **Provider instructions** | No | Shown to customers after they place an order with this method (e.g., bank transfer details) |

### Understanding Drivers

The driver connects a payment method to a payment provider:

| Driver | How it works | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Manual** | No online processing. You handle payment outside of Shopper | Cash on delivery, bank transfer, check |
| **Stripe** | Payments processed via Stripe (requires API keys) | Credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay |

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/payment-methods-driver.png" alt="select payment method driver" />

<Note>
Only **Manual** is built in. Stripe appears in the **Driver** list once your developer has installed the Stripe package, and it requires API credentials configured by your developer. If a driver shows "Not configured" in the dropdown, the API keys have not been set up yet. See the [Payments developer documentation](/2.x/payments) for setup instructions.
</Note>

## Assigning to Zones

Payment methods are assigned to [zones](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones) when creating or editing a zone. A payment method only appears at checkout if it belongs to the customer's zone.

To make a payment method available in a zone:

1. Go to **Settings > Zones**
2. Select the zone and click the pencil icon at the top right of its details
3. In the **Providers** section, select the payment method in the **Payment Methods** field
4. Click **Save**

## Managing Payment Methods

### Editing a Payment Method

Click the pencil icon next to a method, update the fields, and click **Save changes**.

### Enabling or Disabling

Toggle the **Status** switch in the list to quickly enable or disable a payment method. Disabled methods are not shown at checkout.

### Deleting a Payment Method

Click the trash icon and confirm. Orders that used this method are kept, but they no longer show a payment method.

## Common Setups

| Payment Method | Driver | Best For |
|----------------|--------|----------|
| Credit Card | Stripe | Online stores accepting card payments |
| Cash on Delivery | Manual | Local deliveries where customers pay on receipt |
| Bank Transfer | Manual | B2B orders or high-value purchases |
| Mobile Money | Manual | Markets where mobile payments are common (Africa, Asia) |
