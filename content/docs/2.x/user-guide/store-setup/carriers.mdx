Carriers are the shipping providers that deliver your products to customers. You can add carriers like UPS, FedEx, DHL, or your own local delivery service, and configure delivery options with pricing for each [shipping zone](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones).

Shopper supports two types of carriers:

- **Manual carriers** where you set flat-rate prices for each delivery option
- **API-connected carriers** that fetch real-time shipping rates from the carrier's system (UPS, FedEx, USPS)

## Accessing Carriers

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Carriers** from the settings menu

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/carriers-list.png" alt="Carriers list page" />

The carriers list shows all your configured carriers with their driver type, status, and last update date. You can enable or disable a carrier directly from the list using the status toggle.

## Adding a Carrier

To add a new shipping carrier:

1. Click the **Add carrier** button
2. Fill in the carrier details
3. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/carriers-create.png" alt="Add carrier form" />

### Carrier Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Carrier name** | Yes | The carrier's name (e.g., "UPS", "Local Delivery", "Colissimo") |
| **Driver** | Yes | How rates are calculated. Defaults to **Manual** (custom rates per zone). Choose a provider like **UPS** for real-time rates |
| **Website** | No | The carrier's website URL |
| **Description** | No | A short description of the carrier |

### Choosing a Driver

The driver determines how shipping rates are calculated:

| Driver | How it works | When to use |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Manual** | You set fixed prices for each delivery option | Local delivery, flat-rate shipping, free shipping |
| **UPS** | Rates are fetched from the UPS API in real time | You have a UPS account and want dynamic pricing |
| **FedEx** | Rates are fetched from the FedEx API in real time | You have a FedEx account and want dynamic pricing |
| **USPS** | Rates are fetched from the USPS API in real time | You ship within the United States |

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/carriers-driver.png" alt="select carrier driver" />

<Note>
API-connected drivers (UPS, FedEx, USPS) require API credentials to be configured by your developer. If a driver shows a "Not configured" badge in the dropdown, the credentials have not been set up yet. See the [Carriers developer documentation](/2.x/carriers) for setup instructions.
</Note>

## Managing Carriers

### Editing a Carrier

To update a carrier's information:

1. Click the pencil icon next to the carrier you want to modify
2. Update the fields you want to change
3. Click **Save changes**

### Enabling or Disabling a Carrier

You can quickly enable or disable a carrier without editing it by toggling the **Status** switch directly in the carriers list. Disabled carriers are not shown as shipping options during checkout.

### Deleting a Carrier

To remove a carrier:

1. Click the trash icon next to the carrier
2. Confirm the deletion

<Warning>
Deleting a carrier does not remove its delivery options, and a carrier that still has delivery options cannot be deleted. Remove its options from each zone first. If you have active orders using this carrier's shipping, consider disabling it instead of deleting it.
</Warning>

## Setting Up Delivery Options

After creating a carrier, you need to add delivery options with pricing. Delivery options are configured per [zone](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones), so the same carrier can have different options and prices for different regions.

To add a delivery option:

1. Go to **Settings > Zones**
2. Select the zone where you want to add shipping options
3. In the **Shipping Options** section, click **Add Option**
4. In the panel that opens (for example "Shipping Option for the Europe zone"), fill in the delivery details and select the carrier. The **Carriers** list only shows the carriers added to this zone
5. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/zone-delivery-method-form.png" alt="Add delivery option form" />

### Delivery Option Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Yes | The option name shown at checkout (e.g., "Standard Delivery", "Express 24h"). Each name can be used once per zone |
| **Price** | Yes | The shipping price for this option in the zone's currency. Enter 0 for free delivery |
| **Carriers** | Yes | The carrier that provides this delivery option, chosen among the carriers added to the zone |
| **Description** | No | Details about delivery time, up to 200 characters |
| **Visibility** | No | Off by default. Turn it on to make the option available in your store |
| **Metadata** | No | Optional key-value data for custom use |

<Tip>
Create multiple delivery options for the same carrier to offer different speed/price tiers. For example, a carrier could have "Standard (3-5 days)" at $5.99 and "Express (next day)" at $14.99.
</Tip>

### Viewing Delivery Options

All delivery options for a zone are displayed in the zone's detail page under the **Shipping Options** section. Each option shows the carrier logo (when the carrier has one), the option name with an **Enabled** or **Disabled** badge, its description, and its price.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/zone-delivery-methods.png" alt="Zone shipping options list" />

### Editing or Removing Delivery Options

To edit a delivery option, click the pencil icon next to it. To remove it, click the trash icon and confirm.

## Common Setups

Here are typical carrier configurations for different store types:

### Local Business

| Carrier | Option | Price |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Local Delivery | Same-day delivery | $8.00 |
| Local Delivery | In-store pickup | Free |

### National Store

| Carrier | Option | Price |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Standard Shipping | 5-7 business days | $5.99 |
| Express Shipping | 2-3 business days | $12.99 |
| Free Shipping | Free delivery (5-7 business days) | Free |

### International Store

| Carrier | Option | Price |
|---------|--------|-------|
| UPS (API) | Ground, Express, Overnight | Real-time rates |
| DHL | International Express | $24.99 |
| Free Shipping | Free delivery, added in the domestic zone only | Free |

## Free Shipping

To offer free shipping:

1. Create a carrier named "Free Shipping"
2. Add this carrier to the zone's **Carriers** (see [Shipping Zones](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones))
3. Add a delivery option with a **Price** of **0** and use the description to say what it includes (e.g., "Delivered in 5-7 business days")

<Note>
A delivery option has no minimum order amount: a free option is offered on every order in its zone. To limit free shipping to some regions, add the free option only in those zones.
</Note>

<Tip>
You can combine free shipping with paid options. Customers see all available options at checkout and choose the one they prefer.
</Tip>
