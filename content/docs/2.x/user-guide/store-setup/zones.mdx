---
title: Shipping Zones
description: "Define where you ship, which currencies you accept, and which payment and delivery methods are available."
---

A shipping zone is a group of countries that share the same currency, payment methods, and delivery options. For example, you might have a "Europe" zone with EUR currency and Colissimo/DHL delivery, and a "North America" zone with USD currency and UPS/FedEx delivery.

Zones are central to how your store operates. When a customer checks out, Shopper matches their shipping address to a zone and uses it to determine:

- Which **currency** to charge in
- Which **payment methods** to show (Stripe, PayPal, Cash on Delivery)
- Which **delivery options** are available and at what price

Tax rules do not come from the zone: they follow the customer's country and state, as set in [Taxes](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/taxes).

<Note>
You need at least one enabled zone before customers can complete checkout.
</Note>

## Accessing Zones

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Zones** from the settings menu

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/starterkit-zones.png" alt="Shipping Zones list" caption="Zones settings page" />

The zones page lists your zones on the left. Each zone shows its name and code, an **Enabled** or **Disabled** badge, and its carriers and countries. Select a zone to show its details and shipping options on the right.

## Creating a Zone

To create a new zone:

1. Click the **plus icon** next to the **Zones** heading. The **Add zone** panel opens
2. Fill in the zone details
3. Click **Save**. The zones page opens with the new zone selected

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/add-zone.png" alt="Create zone form" />

### Zone Fields

**Payment Methods** and **Carriers** sit in the **Providers** section of the form, where you add the delivery and payment methods available in the zone.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Yes | A descriptive name (e.g., "Europe", "North America", "Africa") |
| **Code** | No | A short identifier code (e.g., "EU", "NA", "AF") |
| **Countries** | Yes | The countries included in this zone. A country can only belong to one zone. |
| **Currency** | Yes | The currency used for pricing and checkout in this zone, chosen among the currencies configured for your store. Each zone has exactly one currency. |
| **Visibility** | No | Whether this zone is active for customers. Disabled zones are not matched during checkout. |
| **Payment Methods** | Yes | Which payment methods are available in this zone (e.g., Stripe, Cash on Delivery) |
| **Carriers** | Yes | Which shipping carriers serve this zone (e.g., UPS, Colissimo, Local Delivery) |
| **Metadata** | No | Optional key-value data for custom use |

<Tip>
Countries already assigned to another zone are grayed out in the country selector. Each country can only belong to one zone.
</Tip>

## Zone Detail Page

Select a zone in the zones list to show its details on the same page. The header shows the zone's name and code, with a pencil icon to edit it and a trash icon to delete it. Below come its currency, countries, payment methods, carriers, and metadata, then the **Shipping Options** section with its delivery options.

<Screenshot src="/images/v2/starterkit-enable-zone.png" alt="Zone detail page" />

## Editing a Zone

To update a zone's settings:

1. Select the zone in the zones list
2. Click the pencil icon at the top right of the zone details
3. Modify the fields you want to change
4. Click **Save**

You can change the zone's name, code, countries, currency, payment methods, and carriers at any time.

<Warning>
Changing a zone's currency affects how prices are displayed to customers in that region. Make sure you have [prices](/2.x/user-guide/marketing/pricing) configured in the new currency before switching.
</Warning>

## What's Configured Inside a Zone

Each zone connects three things together for a region:

| What | Purpose | Where to manage |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Countries** | Which countries belong to this zone | Zone form |
| **Currency** | What currency customers pay in | Zone form |
| **Payment methods** | How customers can pay (Stripe, Cash on Delivery, etc.) | Zone form (select from available methods) |
| **Carriers** | Which shipping providers serve this zone | Zone form (select from available carriers) |
| **Shipping options** | Specific delivery options with pricing | **Shipping Options** section of the zone (see [Carriers](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/carriers)) |
| **Tax rules** | Tax rates applied at checkout. Not part of the zone: they follow the customer's country and state | Managed separately in [tax settings](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/taxes) |

<Tip>
You need to create [carriers](/2.x/user-guide/store-setup/carriers) and payment methods before you can assign them to a zone. Set those up first, then create your zones.
</Tip>

## Zones in Orders

The orders list can show which zone applies to each order. This helps you understand which shipping and payment rules were used. The **Zone** column is hidden by default: turn it on from the **Column manager** above the table. To see only the orders of one zone, open **Filter** and pick a **Zone**.

## Common Zone Setups

### Single-Country Store

If you only sell domestically, create one zone:

| Zone | Countries | Currency | Carriers |
|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Domestic | Your country | Your currency | Local carriers |

### Multi-Region Store

For stores selling across regions:

| Zone | Countries | Currency | Carriers |
|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Domestic | France | EUR | Colissimo, Chronopost |
| Europe | Germany, Belgium, Spain, Italy | EUR | DHL, UPS |
| North America | United States, Canada | USD | FedEx, UPS |
| Africa | Cameroon, Senegal, Ivory Coast | XAF | DHL Express |

### Global Store with Few Zones

Keep it simple with broad zones:

| Zone | Countries | Currency |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Domestic | Your country | Your currency |
| International | All others | USD |
