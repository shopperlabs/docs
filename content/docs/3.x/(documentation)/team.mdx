A team member is a user of your application's `User` model who holds a role other than the customer role, `config('shopper.admin.roles.user')`. Team members and customers share the `users` table: the role decides who opens the admin panel and what they can do there.

Merchants manage the team from Settings, Staff & permissions, at `/cpanel/setting/team` with the default prefix. Roles and permissions are described on the [Permissions](/3.x/acl) page, and the rules that limit what a non-administrator can do on this screen in [Team Management Safeguards](/3.x/acl#team-management-safeguards).

## Team Members

Shopper seeds two team roles, `administrator` and `manager`, and both open the admin panel. The administrator role receives every permission, and the manager role the catalog, sales and customer permissions listed in [Manager Permissions](/3.x/acl#manager-permissions). A member who holds only a role you created, such as a "Fulfillment" role for the warehouse team, opens the admin panel when that role holds the `system.dashboard` permission. The [Roles](/3.x/acl#roles) section lists the seeded roles.

A user can hold several roles. A customer who receives the `manager` role keeps their orders and addresses and appears on the Team screen, because the screen lists every user with at least one role other than the customer role.

## Adding a Team Member

The **Add administrator** button of the Team screen opens a slide-over with three sections:

| Section | Fields |
|---------|--------|
| Login information | Email and Send Invite, or email and password (see below) |
| Personal information | First name, last name, gender, phone number |
| Role information | One or more roles |

The email, the first name, the last name and at least one role are required. The email address must not belong to an existing user: to give a customer access to the admin panel, assign a role to their account, as shown in [Assigning Roles](#assigning-roles).

The role list never shows the customer role. Administrators see every other role. Other users see only the roles they can give: not the administrator role, not a role without any permission, and not a role that holds a permission they lack. Shopper saves the account and its roles in one database transaction, so a failure leaves no account without a role, and marks the new member's email as verified.

The Login information section depends on `password_reset` in `config/shopper/auth.php`:

| `password_reset` | Login information | Password of the new account |
|------------------|-------------------|-----------------------------|
| `true` (default) | Email, **Send Invite** (on by default) | A random password that nobody sees. The member chooses their own through the [welcome email](#welcome-email) or the **Forgot your password?** link of the login page |
| `false` | Email, password (required) | The password typed in the slide-over. The **Generate** link next to the field fills it with a random password of 16 characters. The merchant passes it on to the member |

With `password_reset` set to `false`, the admin has no reset page to link to, so the slide-over hides **Send Invite** and sends no email.

### Preparing the User Model

The slide-over creates the user with Eloquent's `create()` method, so your `User` model must accept its attributes by mass assignment. Declare them with the `Fillable` attribute, along with the other columns Shopper writes, explained on the [Customers](/3.x/customers#user-model-integration) page:

```php title="app/Models/User.php"
use Illuminate\Database\Eloquent\Attributes\Fillable;
use Illuminate\Foundation\Auth\User as Authenticatable;
use Illuminate\Notifications\Notifiable;
use Shopper\Models\Contracts\ShopperUser;
use Shopper\Traits\InteractsWithShopper;

#[Fillable([
    'first_name',
    'last_name',
    'email',
    'password',
    'phone_number',
    'gender',
    'birth_date',
    'opt_in',
    'email_verified_at',
    'avatar_type',
    'avatar_location',
    'last_login_at',
    'last_login_ip',
    'store_two_factor_secret',
    'store_two_factor_recovery_codes',
])]
class User extends Authenticatable implements ShopperUser
{
    use InteractsWithShopper;
    use Notifiable;
}
```

The `Notifiable` trait sends the [welcome email](#welcome-email). Keep the `Hidden` attribute and the other contracts described in [Prepare Your User Model](/3.x/installation#prepare-your-user-model).

### In Code

A seeder or an import adds team members the same way, with the role names read from `config('shopper.admin.roles')`:

```php
use App\Models\User;
use Illuminate\Support\Facades\Hash;
use Illuminate\Support\Str;

$user = User::query()->create([
    'first_name' => 'Jane',
    'last_name' => 'Cooper',
    'email' => 'jane@example.com',
    'password' => Hash::make(Str::password(16)),
    'email_verified_at' => now(),
]);

$user->assignRole(config('shopper.admin.roles.manager'));
```

The `shopper:user` command creates the first administrator from the terminal, as described in [Admin User Creation](/3.x/acl#admin-user-creation).

## Welcome Email

With **Send Invite** on, Shopper asks the default password broker for a reset token with `Password::sendResetLink()` once the member is created, and sends the `Shopper\Notifications\AdminSendCredentials` notification with that token. The email is titled "Welcome to Shopper". It holds the URL of the store from `config('app.url')` and a **Choose my password** button that opens the admin's **Reset password** page (the `shopper.password.reset` route) with the member's email filled in. The member types a password there and Shopper signs them in, or sends them to the login page when the account uses two-factor authentication. The notification goes through the `mail` channel during the request, so the mailer of your application must be configured.

The link expires after the `expire` minutes of the default broker, `auth.passwords.users.expire` in `config/auth.php` (60 in a new Laravel application), and the email states that delay. After that, the member requests a new link with **Forgot your password?** on the login page. The broker also throttles: when a token for the same email is less than `throttle` seconds old, no email is sent, the member is still created, and a warning notification reads "The team member was created, but the invitation email was not sent." with the broker's message below it.

With **Send Invite** off, Shopper sends nothing. The member opens the login page and clicks **Forgot your password?** to choose their password. When `password_reset` is `false`, there is no Send Invite and no reset page: the merchant gives the member the password typed in the slide-over.

The email text comes from the `mails.admin` keys of Shopper's translation files. When the store enables them, a new member who signs in can turn on [two-factor authentication](/3.x/two-factor) or add a passkey from their profile page.

## Assigning Roles

The slide-over gives a member their roles when it creates them. The Team screen does not change the roles of an existing member: change them in code, with the methods of [Assigning Roles](/3.x/acl#assigning-roles).

`syncRoles()` replaces every role of the user, so a manager moving to the warehouse team keeps only the new role:

```php
use App\Models\User;

$user = User::query()->where('email', 'jane@example.com')->firstOrFail();

$user->syncRoles(['fulfillment']);
```

Roles are checked on every request, so the change applies to the member's next page or action in the admin panel. A user who keeps only the customer role, or no role, no longer appears on the Team screen and can no longer open the admin panel. Removing the roles this way keeps the account, unlike deleting it.

## The Team Screen

The Team screen has two parts. The roles part shows a card for each role except the customer role, with the number of accounts that hold it. A card opens the role page, which has three tabs: Role for its name, display name and description, Users for the members who hold it, and Permissions. Users with `system.settings` create a role from the button next to the title.

The Administrators accounts table lists the team members:

| Column | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Full name | The member's name and photo, with a badge on your own row |
| Role | Every role the member holds |
| Joined | The creation date of the account |
| Last signed in | The `last_login_at` column of the user |

Each row has a **Copy user ID** action and a **Delete user** action.

The `shopper:user` command fills `last_login_at` when it creates an administrator, and the sign-in of the admin panel does not update it. To keep the column current, update it from a listener on Laravel's `Login` event, which the admin panel dispatches on every password and passkey sign-in:

```php title="app/Listeners/RecordTeamMemberSignIn.php"
namespace App\Listeners;

use Illuminate\Auth\Events\Login;

class RecordTeamMemberSignIn
{
    public function handle(Login $event): void
    {
        $event->user->forceFill([
            'last_login_at' => now(),
            'last_login_ip' => request()->ip(),
        ])->saveQuietly();
    }
}
```

`saveQuietly()` skips the model events, so a sign-in does not reindex the user in the search engine.

## Removing a Team Member

Only an administrator deletes team members, one at a time with **Delete user** or several at once with the bulk action. Neither action can delete your own account or the last administrator, as listed in [Team Management Safeguards](/3.x/acl#team-management-safeguards).

Deleting a team member deletes the user. Shopper detaches their roles and deletes their addresses first. Orders the user placed as a customer stay in the store, without a customer. To take away access and keep the account, remove the roles instead, as shown in [Assigning Roles](#assigning-roles).

## Permissions

The Team screen checks two system permissions, and keeps two actions for administrators:

| Permission | Allows |
|------------|--------|
| `system.users` | Opening the Team screen and the role pages, listing the members, adding a member |
| `system.settings` | Creating, renaming and deleting roles, granting and revoking permissions |
| Administrator role | Deleting team members, creating and deleting permissions, opening the administrator role |

A user with `system.users` who is not an administrator can give a new member only a role whose permissions they all hold, and never a role without any permission. The [Permissions](/3.x/acl#team-management-safeguards) page explains this rule.

## Customizing the Team Screen

The Team screen, the members table and the slide-over are Livewire components of the `setting` group. The [Components](/3.x/acl#components) section of the Permissions page lists their keys and explains how a replacement keeps the safeguards.

## Events

Shopper dispatches no event of its own when a team member is added or deleted. The `User` model fires Eloquent's `created` and `deleted` events, but `created` runs before the slide-over assigns the roles, so a listener on it cannot tell a team member from a customer. The [Events](/3.x/events) page lists the events Shopper dispatches.

## In the Admin

Merchants open the Team screen from Settings, Staff & permissions. It lists the roles and the team members, adds a member through the Add administrator slide-over, opens each role to edit its permissions, and deletes members. The [User Guide](/3.x/user-guide) walks through these screens.

## From a Storefront

The Store API does not expose team members: no endpoint lists, creates or deletes them. Its registration endpoint, `/store/auth/register`, gives every new account the customer role. Team members cannot use the Store API as customers: an account that can open the admin panel cannot sign in to the Store API or reset its password there, and its store tokens answer `401`. A team member who also buys on the store needs a separate customer account with another email address. The Store API [Authentication](/3.x/store-api/authentication#registering-a-customer) guide covers registration.
