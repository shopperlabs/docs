Staff users are the people who help manage your store. Shopper allows you to create multiple admin accounts with different levels of access.

## Overview

The Staff management section allows you to:

- Add new administrators
- See every staff account with its roles and when it joined
- Assign roles and permissions
- Delete the accounts of people who no longer need access

The section opens to administrators and to staff whose role has the **View Users** permission.

## Accessing Staff Settings

1. Click on **Settings > Site** in the sidebar
2. Select **Staff & permissions** under **Team** in the settings menu

You can also click your avatar at the top-right of the header, then **Manage Users**. Staff who have **View Users** without **Access Settings** have no **Settings** item in the sidebar: the account menu is their way in.

The page opens with the title **Administrators & roles**. It shows your roles as cards at the top (see [Roles & Permissions](/3.x/user-guide/administration/roles)), then the **Administrators accounts** table that lists your staff:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Full Name** | The staff member's picture and name. Your own row shows a **Me** badge |
| **Role** | The roles assigned to the staff member, one badge each |
| **Joined** | The date the account was created |
| **Last signed in** | The last sign-in Shopper recorded, or a dash |

Click **Joined** or **Last signed in** to sort the table by that column. The actions menu at the end of each row holds **Copy user ID**, which copies the account's ID for your developer or your support requests.

## Adding a New Admin

To add a new staff member:

1. Click the **Add administrator** button
2. Under **Login information**, enter the **Email address**. Leave **Send Invite** on to email the new administrator a link to choose their password
3. Under **Personal Information**, enter the **First name** and **Last Name**, and optionally the **Gender** and **Phone number**
4. Under **Role Information**, check one or more roles in **Choose a role for this admin**
5. Click **Save**

The form opens in a panel on the right. To leave without saving, click **Cancel**, the close button or press Escape.

By default, the form has no password field. Shopper gives the account a random password that nobody sees, and the new administrator chooses their own password from the invitation email or from the **Forgot your password?** link of the login page. Their email address is marked as verified when you create it.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/add-admin.png" alt="The Add administrator panel with an email address, Send Invite turned on and two roles checked." />

With **Send Invite** on, the new administrator receives a "Welcome to Shopper" email with a **Choose my password** button. The button opens the **Reset password** page of the admin with their email address filled in: they type their password in **New Password** and **Confirm Password**, click **Update password**, and Shopper signs them in. The link expires after 60 minutes unless your developer changed that delay; after that, they request a new one from the [forgot password page](/3.x/user-guide/getting-started/login#forgot-your-password). Without the invitation, they use that same page to choose a password the first time.

If the invitation cannot be sent, the administrator is still created, and a warning reads "The team member was created, but the invitation email was not sent." with the reason below it. The new administrator can then use the forgot password page.

When your developer turns password resets off, the login page has no **Forgot your password?** link and the form changes: **Send Invite** is hidden, and a **Password** field asks for the password of the new account. Type one or click **Generate** to create one, then give it to the new administrator yourself. Shopper sends no email.

### Admin Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Email address** | Yes | Login email address (must not be used by another account) |
| **Send Invite** | No | On by default. Emails the new administrator a link to choose their password. Hidden when password resets are turned off |
| **Password** | Yes, when shown | Shown only when password resets are turned off. **Generate** fills in a random 16-character password, and the eye icon shows it |
| **First name** | Yes | Staff member's first name |
| **Last Name** | Yes | Staff member's last name |
| **Gender** | No | Male or Female |
| **Phone number** | No | Contact phone number |
| **Choose a role for this admin** | Yes | The roles of this user, picked from a list of checkboxes. Check at least one |

<Note>
Only administrators can assign the **Administrator** role to a new team member. Other staff members only see the roles whose permissions they all hold themselves: never the Administrator role, and never a role that has no permission checked. The **Attention needed** message at the bottom of the form reminds you that the Administrator role gives the same rights and permissions as yours.
</Note>

## Staff Roles

Each staff member is assigned one or more roles that determine what they can access:

| Role | Typical Access |
|------|----------------|
| **Administrator** | Full access to all features. Installed with Shopper |
| **Manager** | Installed with Shopper. Manages the catalog, orders, promotions, campaigns, reviews and customers, without the settings, the staff accounts or the inventory locations, and cannot anonymize customers |
| **Editor** | A role you create for product and content management |
| **Support** | A role you create for orders and customer management |

A staff member with several roles can do everything any of their roles allows. See [Roles & Permissions](/3.x/user-guide/administration/roles) to create roles and choose their permissions.

<Tip>
Create roles that match your team structure. The principle of least privilege suggests giving users only the access they need for their job.
</Tip>

## Managing Existing Staff

### Edit Staff Member

The staff list has no edit action: you cannot change another person's name, email address, password or roles after the account is created. Instead:

- Each staff member updates their own details and password from their profile (see [Your Profile](#your-profile) below)
- To change what someone can do, change the permissions of their role on the [Roles & Permissions](/3.x/user-guide/administration/roles) page. The change applies to everyone with that role

For developers, [Assigning Roles](/3.x/team#assigning-roles) shows how to change the roles of an existing account in code.

### Disable Staff Member

Shopper has no option to disable a staff account and enable it again later. To stop someone from signing in, delete their account as described below. If they come back, add them again with **Add administrator**.

### Delete Staff Member

Permanently remove a staff account:

1. Open the actions menu at the end of the staff member's row in **Administrators accounts** and select **Delete user**. The same menu appears on the **Users** tab of each role
2. Confirm the deletion

To remove several accounts at once, check their rows, click **Delete** above the table and confirm.

- Only administrators can delete staff accounts
- You cannot delete your own account, nor the last account with the Administrator role
- Use with caution: this cannot be undone

## Your Profile

Each staff member manages their own details, password and signed-in devices from **My profile**: see [Your Profile](/3.x/user-guide/getting-started/dashboard#your-profile).

## Security Recommendations

1. **Remove inactive accounts**: delete the accounts of staff who leave
2. **Use role-based access**: don't give everyone admin access
3. **Enable two-factor**: add an extra layer of security with [Two-Factor Authentication](/3.x/user-guide/administration/two-factor)
4. **Monitor activity**: ask staff to check the devices signed in to their account from their profile and log out the sessions they don't recognize. Shopper does not keep a history of staff actions
5. **Regular password changes**: encourage periodic password updates

For developers, see [Team](/3.x/team) and [Permissions](/3.x/acl).
