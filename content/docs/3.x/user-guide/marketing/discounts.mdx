Discounts are a powerful way to attract customers, boost sales, and reward loyalty. Shopper provides flexible discount options for your marketing campaigns.

## Overview

The Promotions section allows you to:

- Create discount codes, or discounts that apply automatically without a code
- Limit a discount to one zone
- Define discount rules and conditions
- Decide which discounts can add up on the same cart
- Track discount usage and the revenue it brings
- Schedule promotional periods

## Accessing Promotions [#accessing-discounts]

Open **Sales > Promotions** in the sidebar. The page lists your discounts, with the **New discount** button at the top.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/discounts.png" alt="The Promotions page listing discounts with their status, usage and campaign." />

The list shows the **Code** (**N/A** for an automatic discount), the **Method**, the **Type**, the **Amount**, what the discount **Applies To**, the **Customer eligibility**, the **Status**, the active **Date**, the **Usage limits**, the **Redemptions** (how many times the discount was used), the **Zone**, and the **Campaign**. Hide the columns you don't need from the column selector. The filters narrow the list by **Active** (the **Visibility** of the discount, not its **Status**), method, campaign, what the discount applies to, customer eligibility, zone, or start date.

Each row has three actions: **Edit**, **Duplicate**, and **Delete**. Select several discounts to delete them together.

### Discount Status

The **Status** column tells you whether customers can use a discount right now:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Customers can use the discount |
| **Scheduled** | The start date is in the future |
| **Expired** | The end date has passed |
| **Limit reached** | The discount was used as many times as its usage limit allows |
| **Disabled** | **Visibility** is turned off |
| **Inapplicable** | The discount applies to specific products, and all of them were deleted |

When several apply, the list shows the first one in this order: **Disabled**, **Expired**, **Limit reached**, **Scheduled**, **Inapplicable**, then **Active**.

### Empty State

If you haven't created any discounts yet, the page shows **Manage discounts and promotions** with a **New discount** button:

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/discounts-empty-state.png" alt="The Promotions page with no discounts and the New discount button." />

## Creating a Discount

To create a new discount:

1. Click the **New discount** button. The **New discount** panel opens with three steps: **Type**, **Conditions**, and **Campaign**
2. In **Type**, choose the type and the value, the method, the code, the zone, and the visibility
3. In **Conditions**, set what the discount applies to, who can use it, the minimum requirements, the dates, the usage limits and, under **Combinations**, how it adds up with other discounts
4. In **Campaign**, attach the discount to a [campaign](/3.x/user-guide/marketing/campaigns) if it belongs to one
5. Click **Save**

The discount is created and its edit page opens. To leave the panel without saving, click its close button or press Escape.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/discount-create.png" alt="The New discount panel on the Type step with a percentage discount code." />

### Discount Types

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Percentage** | A percentage off the order or selected products | 20% off |
| **Fixed amount** | A fixed amount off the order or selected products | $10 off |

<Note>
There is no free shipping discount type. To offer free delivery, add a delivery option with a price of 0 in the zone (see [Free Shipping](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/carriers#free-shipping)).
</Note>

### Discount Configuration

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Type** | **Percentage** or **Fixed amount** |
| **Percentage** or **Fixed amount** | The discount value: from 1 to 100 for a percentage, at least 0.01 for a fixed amount. A fixed amount is stored in cents |
| **Method** | **Discount code**: customers enter a code at checkout to redeem the discount. **Automatic**: the discount applies by itself when the cart matches its conditions, without a code |
| **Code** | For the **Discount code** method only. The code customers enter at checkout (e.g., SUMMER20). Must be unique. Click **Generate** to create a random 10-character code |
| **Zone** | Optional. Limits the discount to one zone. A fixed amount is then in the zone's currency |
| **Visibility** | Turn on to make the discount usable. A discount with **Visibility** off is ignored and shows as **Disabled** |
| **Metadata** | On the edit page, under **Advanced**. Extra key and value pairs your developer can use |

## Discount Conditions

Set rules for when the discount can be used in the **Conditions** step of the panel, or the **Targeting** section of the edit page:

### Minimum Requirements

Under **Minimum requirements**, choose one option, then enter the value:

| Condition | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| **None** | No minimum |
| **Minimum purchase amount** | Order must be at least $50 |
| **Minimum quantity of items** | Cart must have at least 3 items |

### Customer Eligibility

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Everyone** | Anyone can use the discount |
| **Specific customers** | Only the customers you pick with **Browse customers** |

Your developer can add other options to this list, for example to target a group of customers. They appear here with their own fields.

### Product Restrictions

Under **Applies To**, choose:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Entire order** | Applies to everything in the cart |
| **Specific products** | Only the products you pick with **Browse products** |

The product picker lists your published products. To discount a whole collection or brand, pick its products. If you switch a discount back to **Entire order**, the products you picked are removed when you save.

## Usage Limits

Control how the discount can be used:

| Limit | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Limit number of times this discount can be used in total** | Turn on, then enter the maximum in **Usage limit value**. The limit counts uses by all customers together |
| **Limit to one use per customer** | Each customer can use the discount once |

When the total limit is reached, the discount shows as **Limit reached** and stops applying.

## Combining Discounts

A cart can hold several discounts at once, up to 5. The **Combinations** section, collapsed by default, decides which of them add up:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Exclusivity class** | **Order**, **Product**, or **Shipping**. Discounts of different classes always add up |
| **Combinable with other discounts** | Lets this discount add up with another discount of the same class. Both discounts must have it turned on |
| **Priority** | When several discounts of the same class are on a cart, the one with the highest number applies first |

For example, a cart carries SUMMER20 (class **Order**, priority 10) and WELCOME10 (class **Order**, priority 0), neither of them combinable. SUMMER20 applies, and WELCOME10 takes nothing off. A third discount of the **Product** class would still apply on top of SUMMER20.

New discounts start in the **Order** class, combinable, with a priority of 0. The **Shipping** class does not discount the shipping fee: it only places the discount after the other classes.

## Scheduling Discounts

Set active periods for your discounts:

- **Start date**: when the discount becomes active. Required. When you create a discount it cannot be in the past; on the edit page, it can.
- **End date**: when the discount expires. It must be on or after the start date.
- **No end date**: leave **End date** empty and the discount runs indefinitely

A discount with a future start date shows as **Scheduled**, and one past its end date as **Expired**.

## Editing a Discount

Click **Edit** on a discount to open its page, titled with its code (or **Automatic**) and its status. The form is split into **General**, **Configuration**, **Targeting**, **Combinations**, **Campaign**, and **Advanced** sections. Click **Save** to apply your changes, or **Cancel** to return to the list. The **Duplicate** and **Delete** buttons sit at the top.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/discount-edit.png" alt="The edit page of a used discount code with the Rule digest and Performance panels." />

The right side holds two panels:

- **Rule digest** sums up the discount in one place: type, code, zone, what it applies to, who can use it, minimum, usage, active dates, and visibility
- **Performance** shows how the discount did: **Usage**, **Orders**, **Gross revenue**, **Discount cost**, and **AOV with code** (the average order value of the orders that used it). It counts paid orders that are processing or completed, and can take up to a minute to show a new order

Once customers have used a discount, some of its terms stop changing so past orders stay consistent:

- The **Zone** of a fixed-amount discount and the **Campaign** are locked, and their helper text says why
- The code, the type, and the value can no longer change. To offer different terms, duplicate the discount, change the copy, and turn off **Visibility** on the original

### Duplicating a Discount

**Duplicate** copies a discount after you confirm, with its products and customers. The copy gets the code followed by `_COPY`, its **Visibility** turned off, and a usage counter at zero, then its edit page opens so you can finish it.

## Campaigns

To share a budget and usage caps between several discounts, group them in a campaign under **Sales > Campaigns**, then pick it in the **Campaign** step or section. [Campaigns](/3.x/user-guide/marketing/campaigns) explains how to create one and how its budget is used. For developer details, see [Campaigns](/3.x/campaigns).

## Best Practices

<Tip>
Use unique, memorable discount codes that are easy to type. Avoid confusing characters like 0/O or 1/l/I.
</Tip>

- Make codes descriptive, like SUMMER2024 or SAVE10
- Protect your margins with usage limits
- Check the **Performance** panel of each discount to see which ones drive sales
- Use end dates to encourage quick action
- Test a discount on your storefront before promoting it

## Common Discount Examples

| Campaign | Code | Type | Value |
|----------|------|------|-------|
| Welcome offer | WELCOME10 | Percentage | 10% off, one use per customer |
| Spend and save | SAVE10 | Fixed amount | $10 off orders over $50 |
| Flash sale | FLASH25 | Percentage | 25% off, 24 hours only |
| Holiday | HOLIDAY15 | Percentage | 15% off storewide |
| Big basket | None (**Automatic**) | Percentage | 10% off orders of 5 items or more |
