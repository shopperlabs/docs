Taxes are calculated automatically at checkout based on where the customer's order is being shipped. You define tax zones (geographic regions), set percentage rates for each zone, and Shopper calculates the tax on the products and on the shipping of every order.

Shopper supports both **tax-inclusive pricing** (VAT, common in Europe where tax is included in the displayed price) and **tax-exclusive pricing** (sales tax, common in the US where tax is added at checkout).

Tax zones are separate from [shipping zones](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/zones): they follow the country and state of the customer's shipping address, so Shopper calculates tax once the cart has a shipping address.

## Accessing Tax Settings

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Taxes** from the settings menu, under **Selling**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/taxes-list.png" alt="The Taxes page with the tax zones on the left and the France zone's details and rates on the right." />

The page lists your tax zones on the left. Each zone shows its country flag, its name, an **Incl. tax** or **Excl. tax** badge, and its province code when it has one. The first zone is selected when the page opens. Select a zone to show its details and its **Tax Rates** table on the right.

## Creating a Tax Zone

A tax zone maps to a country (and optionally a province/state). To create one:

1. Click the **Add tax zone** button at the top of the page. The **Add tax zone** panel opens
2. Select the **Country**
3. Optionally enter a **Province / State code** in ISO 3166-2 format (e.g., "US-CA" for California)
4. Turn on **Incl. tax** if your prices include tax (**tax-inclusive**), or leave it off if tax is added at checkout (**tax-exclusive**)
5. Click **Save**

To leave the panel without saving, click its close button, **Cancel**, or press Escape.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/tax-zone-form.png" alt="The Add tax zone panel with the country, province code, name and Incl. tax toggle filled in." />

### Tax Zone Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Country** | Yes | The country this tax zone applies to |
| **Province / State code** | No | A province or state code in ISO 3166-2 format, country code first (e.g., "US-CA", "US-NY", "FR-IDF"). Leave empty for country-wide rules |
| **Name** | No | A display name for this zone (e.g., "California", "Île-de-France") |
| **Incl. tax** | No | If ON, prices already include tax (VAT). If OFF, tax is added at checkout (sales tax). The tax zones list shows an **Incl. tax** or **Excl. tax** badge |
| **Tax provider** | No | Leave on **System (default)** to use the rates you set below. Other providers appear here once your developer has enabled them |
| **Metadata** | No | Optional key-value data for custom use |

Shopper picks the tax zone from the customer's shipping address. A province zone applies when the state of the address is exactly the code you entered, such as "US-CA". Otherwise the country-wide zone of that country applies. When the country has no tax zone, no tax is added.

### Tax-Inclusive vs Tax-Exclusive

| Setting | How it works | Common in |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Incl. tax** ON | A product listed at 100 EUR already includes tax. The tax amount is extracted from the price. | Europe (VAT), Australia (GST) |
| **Incl. tax** OFF (**Excl. tax**) | A product listed at $100 has tax added on top at checkout (e.g., $100 + $10 tax = $110). | United States, Canada |

## Editing or Deleting a Tax Zone

Select the zone in the list. Its details show the **Country**, the **Province / State code**, the **Tax behavior**, the **Tax provider** and the metadata. Click the pencil icon to change the zone in the same panel used to create it, or the trash icon to delete it after confirming.

## Adding Tax Rates

Each tax zone needs at least one tax rate. The **default rate** applies to all products in the zone, and to shipping, unless a more specific override exists.

To add a rate:

1. Select a tax zone from the list
2. Click **Add Rate** above the **Tax Rates** table. A panel titled with the tax zone (e.g., "Tax rate for France") opens
3. Enter the **Name**, **Code**, and **Rate**
4. Turn on **Default** if this is the zone's primary rate
5. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/tax-rate-form.png" alt="The Tax rate for France panel with a 20% default rate." />

### Tax Rate Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Yes | The rate name, saved with the tax on each order (e.g., "TVA 20%", "State Sales Tax") |
| **Code** | No | Machine-readable identifier (e.g., "FR_VAT_STANDARD") |
| **Rate** | Yes | The percentage (e.g., 20 for 20%, 5.5 for 5.5%), with up to 4 decimals |
| **Default** | No | If ON, this rate applies to the products and the shipping that don't match a specific override. Keep a single default rate per zone |
| **Combinable** | No | Shown as "Allow this rate to stack with parent zone rates." The built-in tax calculation applies one rate per product, so this setting has no effect unless your developer has connected a tax provider that uses it |
| **Metadata** | No | Optional key-value data for custom use |

The **Tax Rates** table lists each rate with its **Name**, **Code**, **Rate**, **Default**, **Targets** (the number of override targets), and **Combinable** columns. A zone without rates shows **No rates configured**. Use the pencil icon to edit a rate and the trash icon to delete it. The default rate opens in the rate panel; a rate whose **Default** toggle is off opens in the override panel, where you can add targets to it.

### Multiple Rates per Zone

Some countries have different tax rates for different types of products. For example, France has:

| Rate | Percentage | Applies to |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Standard VAT | 20% | Most products |
| Reduced VAT | 5.5% | Food, books |
| Zero VAT | 0% | Certain essential goods |

Create each rate in the same zone. Create the standard rate with **Add Rate** and turn on **Default**: it applies to all products. Create each other rate as a **rate override** and pick the products, product types, categories, or shipping it applies to. A rate that is neither the default nor linked to targets is never applied.

Each product gets one rate. Shopper uses the first override whose targets match the product, and the default rate when none matches. Shipping gets one rate the same way: the first override that targets **Shipping**, or the default rate.

## Rate Overrides

Overrides let you apply a non-default tax rate to specific products, product types, categories, or to shipping. For example, you can set food products to use the 5.5% reduced rate instead of the default 20%.

To create an override:

1. Select a tax zone from the list
2. Click **Create Override** above the **Tax Rates** table. A panel titled with the tax zone (e.g., "Override rate for France") opens
3. Enter the **Name** and **Rate**, and optionally a **Code**. An override is never the default rate, so the form has no **Default** toggle
4. Under **Targets**, click **Add target**, then choose a **Target type** and a **Target value**. Add as many targets as you need
5. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/tax-rate-override.png" alt="The Override rate for France panel with a 5.5% rate and two targets." />

### Override Types

| Type | What it targets | Example |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Product types** | All products of a type (Standard, Virtual, External) | Virtual products with digital tax rate |
| **Products** | A specific product | A book with zero-rated VAT |
| **Categories** | All products in a category | Food category with reduced rate |
| **Shipping** | The shipping of every order in the zone (**All shipping fees**) | Shipping exempt from VAT with a 0% rate |

A value can only be picked once per override. The **Target value** list currently offers only the first 50 products or categories: when the one you need is missing, target its product type instead, or ask your developer.

### Taxing Shipping

Shopper taxes the shipping price of an order at the default rate of the zone, like a product. In a tax-inclusive zone, the shipping price already contains its tax: 6.90 EUR of shipping at 20% VAT contains 1.15 EUR of tax, and the customer still pays 6.90 EUR. In a tax-exclusive zone, the tax is added on top: $5.00 of shipping at 10% sales tax becomes $5.50.

To charge another rate on shipping, or no tax at all, create an override that targets shipping:

1. Select the tax zone from the list
2. Click **Create Override** above the **Tax Rates** table
3. Enter a **Name** (e.g., "Shipping exempt") and the **Rate**: 0 to exempt shipping from tax, or the rate shipping must pay
4. Under **Targets**, click **Add target**, choose **Shipping** as the **Target type** and **All shipping fees** as the **Target value**
5. Click **Save**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/tax-rate-override-shipping.png" alt="The Override rate for France panel with a 0% rate and the Shipping target." />

The tax on shipping is part of the tax of the order: the **Tax** line of the order summary includes it.
