Legal pages are essential for any e-commerce store. They protect both you and your customers by clearly outlining the rules and policies of your business.

## Why Legal Pages Matter

Legal pages help you:

- Comply with local and international regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Protect your business from liability
- Build trust with customers
- Define clear terms for transactions

## Accessing Legal Settings

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Legal** from the settings menu, under **Store**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/legal.png" alt="The Legal policy page on the Privacy policy tab, with its title, Enabled toggle and content." />

The **Legal policy** page opens on the **Privacy policy** tab.

## Available Legal Pages

Shopper allows you to configure the following legal documents, each in its own tab on the **Legal policy** page:

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Refund policy** | Outlines your return and refund procedures |
| **Privacy policy** | Explains how you collect, use, and protect customer data |
| **Terms of use** | Defines the rules and guidelines for using your store |
| **Shipping policy** | Details your shipping methods, times, and costs |

The four pages are created when Shopper is installed. They start enabled, with no content.

## Editing Legal Content

To edit a legal page:

1. Click the tab of the policy you want to edit: **Refund policy**, **Privacy policy**, **Terms of use** or **Shipping policy**
2. Change the **Title** if needed. It is required, up to 150 characters, and each policy needs its own title
3. Write or paste your text in the **Content** editor (required)
4. Turn on **Enabled** when the policy is ready to be shown to customers, or turn it off to hide it
5. Click **Save** to store your changes

Each tab has its own **Save** button, which saves only the policy of that tab.

<Warning>
New pages are enabled with empty content, so the Store API already serves them. Write each policy before you open your store. To hide a policy you have not written yet, enter a short text in **Content** (the editor cannot be saved empty), turn off **Enabled**, and click **Save**.
</Warning>

## Writing Your Legal Pages

### Terms of Service

Your terms, written in the **Terms of use** tab, should include:

- Acceptance of terms
- User responsibilities
- Prohibited activities
- Intellectual property rights
- Limitation of liability
- Dispute resolution

### Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Policy should cover:

- What data you collect
- How you use the data
- How you protect customer data
- Cookie usage
- Third-party services
- Customer rights regarding their data

### Refund Policy

Your Refund Policy should explain:

- Eligibility for refunds
- Timeframe for requesting refunds
- Refund process
- Exceptions
- Exchange options

### Shipping Policy

Your Shipping Policy should explain:

- The countries and regions you ship to
- Order processing times
- Delivery options, times, and costs
- Order tracking
- What happens when a parcel is lost or damaged

<Tip>
Consider consulting with a legal professional to ensure your policies comply with applicable laws in your jurisdiction and the regions where you sell.
</Tip>

## Displaying Legal Pages on Your Storefront

Shopper stores the content of your legal pages and serves the enabled ones through the Store API, with their title, slug, content and last update date. Your developer decides where your website shows them, typically:

- Linked in your website footer
- Shown during checkout when required
- Accessible from customer account pages

<Warning>
Turning on **Enabled** makes a policy available to your storefront, but it only appears on your website once your developer displays it. See [From a Storefront](/3.x/legal#from-a-storefront) and the Store API [Legal Pages](/3.x/store-api/store#legal-pages) endpoints.
</Warning>

For developer details, see the [Legal documentation](/3.x/legal).
