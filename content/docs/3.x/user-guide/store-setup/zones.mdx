title: Shipping Zones
description: "Define where you ship, which currencies you accept, and which payment and delivery methods are available."
---

A shipping zone is a group of countries that share the same currency, payment methods, and delivery options. For example, you might have a "Europe" zone with EUR currency and Colissimo/DHL delivery, and a "North America" zone with USD currency and UPS/FedEx delivery.

Zones are central to how your store operates. Your storefront tells Shopper which zone the customer shops in by sending the zone's **Code** with its Store API requests. When it sends none, the default zone set by your developer applies. Only enabled zones are used. Shopper then uses the zone to determine:

- Which **currency** to charge in
- Which **payment methods** to show (Stripe, PayPal, Cash on Delivery)
- Which **delivery options** are available and at what price

Your developer can read how the zone is picked in [Resolving a Zone](/3.x/zones#resolving-a-zone).

Tax rules do not come from the zone: they follow the customer's country and state, as set in [Taxes](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/taxes).

<Note>
You need at least one enabled zone before customers can choose a delivery option. Without a zone, the cart uses your default currency and offers every enabled payment method whose driver is configured, but it gets no shipping options.
</Note>

## Accessing Zones

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Zones** from the settings menu, under **Shipping**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/starterkit-zones.png" alt="The Zones page with the list of zones on the left and the selected zone on the right." />

The zones page lists your zones on the left. Each zone shows its code, its name, an **Enabled** or **Disabled** badge, its number of countries with its currency code, and the flags of up to eight of its countries. Type in **Search zone or country** to find a zone by its name or by the name of one of its countries.

The first zone is selected when the page opens. Select another zone to show its details and shipping options on the right.

## Creating a Zone

To create a new zone:

1. Click **Add zone** at the top of the page. The **Add zone** panel opens
2. Fill in the zone details
3. Click **Save**. The zones page opens with the new zone selected

To leave the panel without saving, click its close button, **Cancel**, or press Escape.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/add-zone.png" alt="The Add zone panel with the name, code, countries, currency and providers filled in." />

### Zone Fields

**Payment Methods** and **Carriers** sit in the **Providers** section of the form, where you add the delivery and payment methods available in the zone.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Yes | A descriptive name (e.g., "Europe", "North America", "Africa") |
| **Slug** | No | A readable identifier for the zone. When you leave it empty, Shopper builds it from the name |
| **Code** | Yes | A short identifier code of up to 10 characters (e.g., "EU", "NA", "AF"). Each zone needs its own code. Your storefront sends this code to pick the zone, so tell your developer before you change it |
| **Countries** | Yes | The countries included in this zone. A country can only belong to one zone. |
| **Currency** | Yes | The currency used for pricing and checkout in this zone, chosen among the currencies configured for your store. Each zone has exactly one currency. |
| **Visibility** | No | Whether this zone is active for customers. Disabled zones are never used by the storefront. |
| **Payment Methods** | Yes | Which payment methods are available in this zone (e.g., Stripe, Cash on Delivery) |
| **Carriers** | Yes | Which shipping carriers serve this zone (e.g., UPS, Colissimo, Local Delivery) |
| **Metadata** | No | Optional key-value data for custom use |

The **Payment Methods** and **Carriers** lists also show the methods and carriers you have disabled. You can add them to the zone, but customers only see the enabled ones.

<Tip>
Countries already assigned to another zone are grayed out in the country selector. Each country can only belong to one zone.
</Tip>

## Zone Detail Page

Select a zone in the zones list to show its details on the same page. The header shows the zone's name and code, with a pencil icon to edit it and a trash icon to delete it. Below come its currency, countries, payment methods, carriers, and metadata, then the **Shipping Options** section with its delivery options.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/starterkit-enable-zone.png" alt="The detail of a zone with its currency, countries, providers and shipping options." />

## Editing a Zone

To update a zone's settings:

1. Select the zone in the zones list
2. Click the pencil icon at the top right of the zone details
3. Modify the fields you want to change
4. Click **Save**

You can change the zone's name, code, countries, currency, payment methods, and carriers at any time.

<Warning>
Changing a zone's currency affects how prices are displayed to customers in that region. Make sure you have [prices](/3.x/user-guide/marketing/pricing) configured in the new currency before switching.
</Warning>

## What's Configured Inside a Zone

Each zone connects three things together for a region:

| What | Purpose | Where to manage |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Countries** | Which countries belong to this zone | Zone form |
| **Currency** | What currency customers pay in | Zone form |
| **Payment methods** | How customers can pay (Stripe, Cash on Delivery, etc.) | Zone form (select from available methods) |
| **Carriers** | Which shipping providers serve this zone | Zone form (select from available carriers) |
| **Shipping options** | Specific delivery options with pricing | **Shipping Options** section of the zone (see [Carriers](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/carriers)) |
| **Tax rules** | Tax rates applied at checkout. Not part of the zone: they follow the customer's country and state | Managed separately in [tax settings](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/taxes) |

<Tip>
You need to create [carriers](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/carriers) and [payment methods](/3.x/user-guide/store-setup/payment-methods) before you can assign them to a zone. Set those up first, then create your zones.
</Tip>

## Zones in Orders

The orders list can show which zone applies to each order. This helps you understand which shipping and payment rules were used. The **Zone** column is hidden by default: turn it on from the **Column manager** above the table. To see only the orders of one zone, open **Filter** and pick a **Zone**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/orders-with-zone.png" alt="The orders list with the Zone column shown and the Zone filter open." />

## Common Zone Setups

### Single-Country Store

If you only sell domestically, create one zone:

| Zone | Code | Countries | Currency | Carriers |
|------|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Domestic | Your country code | Your country | Your currency | Local carriers |

### Multi-Region Store

For stores selling across regions:

| Zone | Code | Countries | Currency | Carriers |
|------|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Domestic | FR | France | EUR | Colissimo, Chronopost |
| Europe | EU | Germany, Belgium, Spain, Italy | EUR | DHL, UPS |
| North America | NA | United States, Canada | USD | FedEx, UPS |
| Africa | AF | Cameroon, Senegal, Ivory Coast | XAF | DHL Express |

### Global Store with Few Zones

Keep it simple with broad zones:

| Zone | Code | Countries | Currency |
|------|------|-----------|----------|
| Domestic | DOM | Your country | Your currency |
| International | INT | All others | USD |
