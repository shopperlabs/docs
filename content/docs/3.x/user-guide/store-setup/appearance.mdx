The Appearance page sets the color theme of your admin panel. A theme changes the colors of the top bar, the sidebar, the pages and the buttons: menus and pages stay where they are. The theme you save applies to every staff member, and it does not change your storefront, which has its own design.

## Accessing Appearance

1. Click on **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select **Appearance** from the settings menu, under **Store**

The page opens with the title **Appearance**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/appearance.png" alt="The Appearance page with the Default, Midnight and Evergreen theme cards, Default selected." />

Under the **Themes** tab, each card shows a small picture of the admin panel in the theme's colors, with the theme's name and its author, such as "Default by Shopper". The card of the current theme has a colored outline.

## Built-in Themes

Shopper comes with three themes:

| Theme | Colors |
|-------|--------|
| **Default** | Neutral grays with a blue accent |
| **Midnight** | A navy top bar, slate grays and a sky blue accent |
| **Evergreen** | Stone grays with a sage green accent |

Your developer can add themes of their own, for example in your brand colors. They appear as extra cards on this page.

## Changing the Theme

To try a theme, then keep it:

1. Click a theme card. The admin panel switches to its colors right away, so you can see how it looks before you decide
2. Click **Save** to keep it. A notification confirms "Theme updated successfully."

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/appearance-preview.png" alt="The Midnight theme previewed before saving, with its navy top bar and the Midnight card selected." />

Until you click **Save**, the preview only shows on your screen. If you leave the page without saving, the admin panel goes back to the saved theme.

<Warning>
Saving changes the theme for every staff member, not only for you. Agree on a theme with your team before you change it.
</Warning>

## Light and Dark Mode

Dark mode is separate from the theme. Each staff member switches between **Light**, **Dark** and **System** in the account menu, under their profile picture in the top-right corner (see [Logging Out](/3.x/user-guide/getting-started/login#logging-out), which describes this menu). The choice is kept in their browser and changes nothing for the others.

Each built-in theme has a light and a dark version. The theme cards follow the mode you are in, so you can compare the themes in the mode you use.

The Appearance page is part of the settings: only staff members with the **Access Settings** permission can open it (see [Roles & Permissions](/3.x/user-guide/administration/roles)).

For developer details on the theme configuration and on creating a theme of your own, see the [Themes documentation](/3.x/themes).
