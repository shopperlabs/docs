Your customers are essential to your business. Shopper helps you manage customer information, track their orders, and build lasting relationships.

## Overview

The Customers section allows you to:

- Follow how many customers you have, how many bought from you, and what they spend on average
- View all registered customers
- Access customer details and order history
- See customer addresses
- Invite the customers you create to choose their password
- Create customers manually
- Anonymize a customer who asks for their data to be removed

## Accessing Customers

Open **Customers > Customers** in the sidebar. The page title reads **Customers**, with the **New customer** button on the right.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customers.png" alt="The Customers page with its four stat cards above the customer list." />

Four stat cards sit above the list, in your store currency:

| Card | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Total customers** | All registered accounts |
| **New customers** | Accounts created in the last 30 days |
| **Active customers** | Customers who placed at least one paid order, with their share of all customers |
| **Average lifetime value** | Average revenue per active customer |

The figures are cached for a few minutes, so a new customer or order can take a moment to show up in them.

## Customer List

The customer list displays:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Full Name** | Customer's full name and picture |
| **Email address** | Contact email address, with an icon when the email is verified |
| **Country** | Country of the customer's first address |
| **Order Placed** | Number of paid orders that are processing or completed |
| **Registered At** | When they registered |

You can filter the list by **E-mail Verified** (**Yes** or **No**) and by registration date: pick the first day of the period in **From** and the last day in **to**. Both days are included, and you can fill only one of the two.

### Empty State

If you don't have any customers yet, the page shows **Manage customer orders & details** with a **New customer** button:

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customers-empty-state.png" alt="The Customers page with no customers and the New customer button." />

## Customer Details

Click a customer's name, or the eye icon (**View**), to open their profile. The header shows their picture and name, an **Active** or **Inactive** badge (active means at least one paid order), and their **Customer ID**. Once they have paid orders, it also shows the total they spent in your store currency, their number of orders, and when they placed the last one. On the right, the **Anonymize customer** button and the previous and next arrows move you to the neighboring customers.

Below the header, the **Profile**, **Orders**, and **Addresses** tabs fill the left side. The right side holds three cards:

- **Customer details**. The **Customer ID** (click **Copy customer ID** to copy it), the **Created** date, whether the **Email** is **Verified**, whether the customer is **Subscribed** to **Marketing emails**, and whether **Two-factor auth** is **Enabled**
- **Contact information**. Email and phone number, or **No phone number on file**
- **Default address**. The customer's default shipping address, or their default billing address when they have no shipping address

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customer-information.png" alt="A customer profile with the Profile tab open and the Customer details, Contact information and Default address cards." />

### Profile Information

The **Profile** tab is read-only. It shows the first name, last name, picture, email address, birth date, and gender (when set). The customer updates this information from their account on your storefront.

### Addresses

The **Addresses** tab lists the customer's addresses in two sections, **Shipping addresses** and **Billing addresses**, with the name, street, postal code, city, country, and phone number of each. The default address of each section carries a **Default** marker. A section without any address shows **No shipping address** or **No billing address**.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customer-address.png" alt="The Addresses tab with a default shipping address and a billing address." />

Addresses cannot be edited from the admin. When you create a customer, the **Address** section of the **New customer** form records their first address, which becomes their default shipping address. Customers add their other addresses and choose their default shipping and billing addresses from your storefront (see [Addresses](/3.x/store-api/customers#addresses) in the Store API).

### Order History

The **Orders** tab lists all orders placed by the customer in a table with the columns **#**, **Date**, **Status** (payment and shipping badges), **Price amount**, and **Purchased**. The **Zone** and **Channel** columns are hidden by default. Filter the orders by status, payment status, zone, or channel, and click **View details** on a row to open the order.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customer-orders.png" alt="The Orders tab of a customer profile with three orders." />

## Creating a Customer

To add a customer yourself, for example after a phone order:

1. Click the **New customer** button on the Customers page
2. Fill in the customer details
3. Click **Save**

The **New customer** page creates the customer account and can email the customer an invitation to choose their password.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/create-customer.png" alt="The top of the New customer form with the Profile overview section and the start of the Address section." />

### Customer Fields

The form has three sections: **Profile overview**, **Address**, and **Notifications**.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **First name** | Yes | Customer's first name |
| **Last Name** | Yes | Customer's last name |
| **Email address** | Yes | Unique email address |
| **Phone number** | No | Contact phone number |
| **Gender** | No | Male or Female |
| **Street Address** | Yes | First line of the customer's address |
| **Apartment, suite, etc.** | No | Second line of the address |
| **City** | Yes | City |
| **State** | No | State or region |
| **Postal / Zip code** | Yes | Postal code |
| **Country** | Yes | Country |
| **Phone number** (address) | No | Phone number for this address |
| **Customer agreed to receive marketing emails.** | No | Records the customer's consent, shown as **Subscribed** next to **Marketing emails** on their profile |
| **Send an invitation email.** | No | Emails the customer a link to choose their password. Shown only when your store has a password reset page |

The form has no password field. Shopper gives the account a random password that nobody sees, and the customer chooses their own password from the invitation email or from the forgot password page of your store. The customer's email is marked as verified on creation, the address is saved as their shipping address, and you return to the customer list.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customer-create.png" alt="The bottom of the New customer form with the Address and Notifications sections." />

## Customer Notification

The admin sends customers one email: an invitation to choose their password. When you create a customer, the **Notifications** section of the form has two checkboxes:

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/customer-notification.png" alt="The Notifications section with the marketing consent and invitation checkboxes." />

With **Send an invitation email.** checked, the customer receives a welcome email ("Welcome to" followed by your store name) with a **Choose my password** button. The button opens the password reset page of your store. The link expires after 60 minutes unless your developer changed that delay; after that, the customer requests a new one from the forgot password page. Without the invitation, the customer uses that same forgot password page to choose a password the first time. **Customer agreed to receive marketing emails.** sends nothing: it only records consent.

**Send an invitation email.** only shows when your storefront has a password reset page that Shopper can link to. If it is missing from the form, ask your developer to set one up (see [Mail Notifications](/3.x/notifications#mail-notifications)).

If the invitation cannot be sent, the customer is still created, and a warning reads "The customer was created, but the invitation email was not sent." with the reason below it. The customer can then use the forgot password page of your store.

Other emails customers expect do not come from the admin:
- Order confirmations: not sent by Shopper; your developer adds them to your store
- Shipping updates: not sent by Shopper; your developer adds them to your store
- Marketing emails: sent from your own email marketing tool, to customers whose profile shows **Subscribed**
- Password resets and email verification: sent by your storefront (the [Livewire starter kit](/3.x/livewire-starter-kit) handles both)

## Customer Data Privacy

If a customer requests their data to be removed (GDPR "right to be forgotten"), you can anonymize their account:

1. Go to the customer's profile page
2. Click **Anonymize customer**
3. Confirm with **Yes, anonymize**

This renames the customer "Deleted Customer", replaces their email with an anonymous address, clears their phone number, gender, picture, birth date, and two-factor settings, withdraws their marketing consent, and deletes all their addresses. Order history is preserved for your records, but the customer's identity is removed. You then return to the customer list.

The **Anonymize customer** button needs the permission to delete customers.

<Warning>
Anonymization cannot be undone. The customer's personal data is permanently replaced. Only use this when a customer explicitly requests data removal.
</Warning>
