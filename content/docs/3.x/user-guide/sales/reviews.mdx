Customer reviews build trust and help other shoppers make purchasing decisions. Shopper allows you to manage and moderate reviews on your products.

## Overview

The Reviews section allows you to:

- View all product reviews
- Approve, reject, or flag reviews as spam
- Monitor review ratings and how many customers recommend your products

## Accessing Reviews

Open **Customers > Reviews** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Customer Reviews**: "See what customers are saying about your products."

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/reviews.png" alt="The Customer Reviews page with rating stats above the list of reviews." />

Above the list, four stat cards and two panels sum up what customers think. They count every review, published or pending:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Average rating** | The average star rating, based on all reviews |
| **Total reviews** | The number of reviews, and how many came in the last 30 days |
| **5-star reviews** | The share of reviews with 5 stars |
| **Pending moderation** | The reviews waiting for you, or **Inbox is clear** when there are none |

The **Ratings breakdown** panel shows how many reviews each star rating received, and **Recommended** shows the share of customers who recommend the product they reviewed.

## Review List

The review list displays all reviews submitted by customers. The **All**, **Pending**, and **Approved** tabs above it split the reviews by status.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Customer** | Who wrote the review |
| **Product** | The reviewed product. Click it to open the product |
| **Rating** | Star rating (1-5) |
| **Review** | The start of the review text, in full when you hover it. Hidden by default: show it from the column selector |
| **Status** | **Published** or **Pending** |
| **Date** | When the review was submitted |

Click the filter icon to narrow the list by rating, product, customer, or whether the customer recommends the product.

### Empty State

If you don't have any reviews yet, the page shows **Customers reviews**:

## Review Moderation

### Review Workflow

1. **Customer submits review**. Your storefront sends the review to your store, and it arrives as **Pending**. Shopper has no review form of its own: your developer adds it (see [Review Submission](/3.x/reviews#review-submission))
2. **Review enters queue**. Pending reviews wait under the **Pending** tab and in the **Pending moderation** count
3. **Admin reviews**. Open the review and approve it, reject it, or mark it as spam
4. **Published**. Approved reviews are the only ones the Store API sends to your storefront (see [Reviews](/3.x/store-api/catalog#reviews) in the Store API)

### Moderation Actions

Click **View** on a review to open it in a panel. The buttons at the bottom of the panel moderate it, and the **Delete** action stays in the list.

| Action | Where | Effect |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **Approve** | Panel of a pending review | The review becomes **Published** |
| **Reject** | Panel of a published review | The review goes back to **Pending** and is no longer published |
| **Mark as spam** | Panel of any review | After you confirm, the review is hidden from your customers and goes back to **Pending** |
| **Delete** | Row action, or bulk action on the selected reviews | After you confirm, the review is permanently removed |

A rejected review and a review marked as spam both return to the **Pending** tab, next to the reviews you haven't moderated yet. Delete the reviews you never want to publish, so the **Pending** tab only holds the reviews still waiting for you.

## Review Details

The **Review** panel shows:

- **Product**. The reviewed product and its SKU
- **Rating**. Star rating from 1 to 5
- **Content**. The review headline and its full text
- **Reviewer**. The customer's picture, name, and email address
- **Created At**. Submission date
- **Approved status**. **Published** or **Pending**

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/review-detail.png" alt="The Review panel of a pending review with the Approve and Mark as spam buttons." />

To close the panel, click its close button or press Escape.

## Review Guidelines

Consider establishing review guidelines for your store:

### What to Approve

- Honest product feedback (positive or negative)
- Helpful information for other shoppers

### What to Reject

- Spam or promotional content (use **Mark as spam**)
- Offensive language or personal attacks
- Reviews for wrong products
- Fake or misleading reviews
- Reviews containing personal information

## Impact of Reviews

Reviews affect your business in several ways:

| Impact | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Trust** | Positive reviews build credibility |
| **SEO** | Review content helps search rankings |
| **Conversion** | Products with reviews convert better |
| **Feedback** | Learn what customers like/dislike |

## Best Practices

<Tip>
Encourage reviews by sending follow-up emails after purchase, making the review process easy, and showing appreciation for customer feedback. Shopper does not send these emails: use your email marketing tool, or ask your developer to add them.
</Tip>

- Moderate consistently: apply the same standards to all reviews
- Learn from feedback: use negative reviews to improve products
- Showcase reviews: display reviews prominently on product pages
