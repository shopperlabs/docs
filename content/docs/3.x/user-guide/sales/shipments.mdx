title: Shipments

A shipment is a package you created from an order with **Create Shipping Label** (see [Creating a Shipment](/3.x/user-guide/sales/orders#creating-a-shipment)). Once it exists, you follow it from **Sales > Orders > Shipments**: this page lists every shipment of your store, shows where each package is, and lets you record its pickup, delivery or return.

## Shipment List

Open **Sales > Orders** in the sidebar, then click **Shipments** below it. The newest shipments come first.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/shipments.png" alt="The Shipments page with its status tabs and a list of UPS shipments: one label created, one out for delivery with its Mark as delivered button, one delivery failed, three in transit and the others delivered." />

| Column | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Shipment ID** | The shipment number, such as SHP-47 |
| **Shipment Event** | A five-step tracker of the package, from label created to delivered |
| **Status** | The current status of the package |
| **Shipped at** | The date the carrier picked up the package |
| **Order Number** | The order the package belongs to. Click it to open the order |
| **Carrier Service** | The carrier that delivers the package, with its logo |

The search box finds a shipment by its order number, or by its shipment ID, with or without the SHP- prefix (type SHP-47 or 47).

### Tabs

The tabs above the table narrow the list by status. Each tab shows how many shipments it holds.

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| **All** | Every shipment, returned ones included |
| **Label created** | Shipments whose label is created but that the carrier has not picked up yet |
| **In transit** | Shipments that are picked up, in transit, at a sorting center, or out for delivery |
| **Delivered** | Shipments that reached their destination |
| **Delivery failed** | Shipments the carrier could not deliver |

### Filters

Click the filter icon next to the search box to narrow the list by **Status** (pick several at once), by **Order Number**, or by **Carrier Service**.

## Shipment Detail

To see everything about a package, click the sliders icon (**Manage shipment**) on its row. The detail panel opens on the right.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/shipment-detail.png" alt="The detail panel of shipment SHP-47, in transit with UPS, showing its tracker, its two products and three tracking events." />

The top of the panel shows the shipment ID with its status, the date it was shipped and a link to its order. Below, a gray box shows the order's shipping address and the carrier.

**Tracking progress**. A 5-step tracker:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Label created">
    The shipping label is created and the package waits for the carrier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Picked up">
    The carrier has collected the package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In transit">
    The package is on its way. A package at a sorting center also stands on this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Out for delivery">
    The package is with the local delivery driver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivered">
    The package has reached the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When a delivery failed or the package was returned, the whole tracker turns red.

**Shipment details**. The **Shipped at** and **Received at** dates, and the **Tracking number**. When the shipment has a tracking URL, the number links to the carrier's tracking page.

**Products**. The items in this package, each with its fulfillment status, such as **Shipped** or **Delivered**.

**Shipment Timeline**. Every event of the package, oldest first. Each event shows its status and date, and its location and description when they were recorded.

To close the panel, click its close button or press Escape.

## Editing the Tracking Details

To fix the carrier or add a tracking number you received later, click the pencil icon (**Edit**) on the shipment's row. The **Edit** window has three fields:

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Carrier Service** | Yes | The carrier that delivers the package, chosen among your carriers |
| **Tracking number** | No | The number the carrier gave the package, such as `1Z999AA10123456784` |
| **Tracking URL** | No | The full address of the carrier's tracking page for this package |

Click **Submit** to save the changes. This does not change the status of the package: record an event for that.

## Adding Tracking Events

Tracking events keep the timeline in step with what the carrier tells you, and they move the order forward: recording **Picked up** or **Delivered** updates the order's shipping status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shipment">
    Click the sliders icon (**Manage shipment**) on the shipment's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add event">
    The button sits next to **Shipment Timeline**. The **Add event** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the event">
    Choose the **Status**, check **Occurred at**, and add a **Location** and a **Description** if you have them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The event appears in the timeline and the shipment takes its status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/shipment-add-event.png" alt="The Add event panel for an in transit shipment, with Out for delivery selected, a date, a location and a description." />

**Occurred at** starts at the current date and time, and cannot be earlier than the last event of the shipment. The **Status** buttons only offer the statuses that can follow the current one:

| Current status | Statuses you can record next |
|----------------|------------------------------|
| **Label created** | **Picked up**, **Returned** |
| **Picked up** | **In transit**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivery failed**, **Returned** |
| **In transit** | **At sorting center**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivery failed**, **Returned** |
| **At sorting center** | **In transit**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivery failed**, **Returned** |
| **Out for delivery** | **Delivered**, **Delivery failed**, **Returned** |
| **Delivery failed** | **In transit**, **Out for delivery**, **Returned** |
| **Delivered** | **Returned** |
| **Returned** | None: the **Add event** button disappears |

Some statuses do more than add a line to the timeline:

- **Picked up** records the shipping date, marks the package's items as **Shipped**, and updates the order's shipping status. A **New** order moves to **Processing**
- **Delivered** has the same effect as [Mark as delivered](#marking-as-delivered)
- **Returned** records the return date and cancels the package's items

<Warning>
Recording **Returned** does not refund the customer and does not put the products back in stock. Refund the payment from the order page as described in [Refunds](/3.x/user-guide/sales/refunds), and adjust the stock of the products yourself.
</Warning>

Shopper does not email the customer when you record an event.

### Automatic Tracking Updates

When the shipment's carrier uses the FedEx or UPS driver with its credentials set, and the shipment has a tracking number, Shopper asks the carrier for new scans every 30 minutes and adds them to the timeline as events. It stops once the package is **Delivered** or **Returned**. This relies on the scheduler and the queue worker your developer runs on the server. Manual and USPS carriers never update on their own: record their events yourself.

## Marking as Delivered

When the customer has received a package that is **Out for delivery**, a **Mark as delivered** button appears on its row in the **Shipments** table. Click it, then click **Confirm** in the dialog that asks whether you are sure. **Cancel** closes the dialog without changing anything.

<Screenshot src="/images/v3/shipment-mark-delivered.png" alt="The Mark as delivered confirmation dialog open over the Shipments table, with its Cancel and Confirm buttons, for shipment SHP-49 which is out for delivery." />

Once confirmed, the shipment and its items become **Delivered**, and the delivery date is recorded.

When every item of the order is delivered, the order's shipping status becomes **Delivered**, and a **Processing** order that is paid moves to **Completed** on its own. The full journey of an order is described in [Typical Order Flow](/3.x/user-guide/sales/orders#typical-order-flow).

<Note>
Browsing the shipments and opening their details only needs access to orders. Adding an event, marking a package as delivered and editing its tracking details need the permission to edit orders: without it, the **Add event** button does not show in the shipment details. See [Roles & Permissions](/3.x/user-guide/administration/roles).
</Note>

For developer details, see [Fulfillment](/3.x/fulfillment).
