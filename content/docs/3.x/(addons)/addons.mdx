---

Add-ons are installed with Composer next to Shopper. Payments: Stripe. Shipping: FedEx, UPS and USPS, which 3.0 ships as opt-in packages.

Until an add-on is covered here, the [2.x Stripe page](/2.x/addons/stripe) describes the payment add-on as it was in 2.x.
