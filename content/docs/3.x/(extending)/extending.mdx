Extending covers the admin panel (control panel, navigation, pages, render hooks and components), the sidebar package, swapping models, payment drivers and shipping carriers, and building an add-on.

Until a topic is covered here, the [2.x Extending section](/2.x/extending/overview) describes the admin panel as it was in 2.x.
